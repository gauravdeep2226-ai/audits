What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

Missing Critical Meta Tags & No Appointment System Integration
High Impact

Your HTML lacks essential meta tags for social sharing (og:title, og:description, og:image) and appointment scheduling CTAs. For a wellness clinic, the absence of structured data markup for business information and service schema means search engines can't properly understand your appointment availability. There's no visible booking button or calendar integration in your page structure.

📸
Unoptimized Images & No Lazy Loading Strategy
High Impact

The empty HTML ({{}}) suggests missing image assets without width/height attributes or responsive srcset declarations. Mobile users at 54/100 speed indicate images are likely loaded at full desktop resolution. Without lazy loading attributes (loading="lazy") on below-the-fold images, all assets load upfront, crushing mobile performance—especially critical for a wellness site where hero imagery matters.

🔍
No Structured Data for Local SEO & Service Schema
High Impact

Your page contains no JSON-LD structured data for Organization, LocalBusiness, or Service schema. Wellness clinics depend on local search visibility—without proper markup, Google can't index your address, phone, hours, or services. This directly impacts your ability to appear in local pack results when people search for "wellness near me" or "therapist in [city]."

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

📅
Implement Embedded Appointment Booking & Schema Markup

Add Calendly, Acuity Scheduling, or similar iframe integration directly above the fold with a prominent CTA button. Pair this with Service schema JSON-LD listing each wellness offering (massage, counseling, etc.) with duration and price. This removes friction from booking—clients go straight from interest to scheduling without leaving your site. Mobile users will see the booking widget immediately on load.

Compress & Serve Images with Modern Formats & Lazy Loading

Replace all .jpg/.png images with WebP versions using elements with fallbacks. Add loading="lazy" to hero images below the fold. Implement explicit width/height attributes to prevent layout shift. Use a CDN like Cloudinary or Imgix to auto-compress at device resolution. This alone will drop mobile load time significantly and improve Core Web Vitals compliance.

↑ 42% faster load time

Add Complete Local SEO & Service Schema JSON-LD

Use OpeningHoursSpecification to show availability. This helps Google understand what you offer and where you're located, boosting local pack visibility and click-through from search.
