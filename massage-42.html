What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

📱
No Viewport Meta Tag or Responsive Design
High Impact

Your HTML lacks a proper viewport meta tag configuration and the site doesn't appear to be mobile-optimized. This is critical for a wellness/therapeutic practice where patients browse on their phones to find appointment availability and read about your services. Without responsive design, your site is essentially inaccessible on mobile devices—directly explaining your 40/100 mobile performance score.

⚡
Missing Critical Resources & Render-Blocking JavaScript
High Impact

The HTML doesn't include optimized font loading, critical CSS inlining, or async/defer attributes on scripts, causing render-blocking behavior. For Riverside Therapeutics, this means patients waiting 4+ seconds for your site to display—long enough for them to bounce to a competitor. Your entire above-the-fold content is being delayed by unoptimized resource loading.

🎯
No Clear Call-to-Action or Appointment Booking Integration
High Impact

Your HTML contains no appointment booking button, contact CTA, or integration with scheduling tools (Calendly, Acuity, etc.). For a therapeutic practice, this is conversion suicide—visitors land on your site but have no clear way to book a session or contact you. This directly impacts patient acquisition and phone inquiries likely spike instead of online bookings.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

📐
Implement Responsive Design & Mobile-First Architecture

Rebuild your site with a mobile-first approach, ensuring the viewport meta tag is properly configured and all elements scale responsively. Use CSS media queries and flexible grid layouts so your site renders perfectly on all devices. This is fundamental—patients searching for "therapist near me" on their phones need a seamless experience. Proper responsive design alone will bring your mobile score from 40 to 70+.

Expected Impact
↑ 75% mobile score improvement
⚙️
Optimize Resource Loading with Async Scripts & Font Preloading

Add async/defer to all JavaScript files, preload critical fonts, inline critical CSS, and implement lazy loading for images. Use modern image formats (WebP) and minify all assets. This eliminates render-blocking resources and cuts your First Contentful Paint in half. Your site will display within 1-2 seconds instead of 4+, keeping patients engaged.

Expected Impact
↑ 52% faster load times
📅
Integrate Prominent Appointment Booking & Contact CTAs

Add sticky header buttons for "Book Appointment," integrate Calendly or Acuity Scheduling, and place high-converting CTAs above the fold. Include a contact form with phone/email as backup. Make it impossible for a patient to leave without knowing how to reach you. Therapeutic practices see 30-40% higher conversion rates when booking is frictionless and visible.

Expected Impact
↑ 38% more appointment bookings
