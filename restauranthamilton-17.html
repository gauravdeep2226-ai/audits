Issues Found

Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Mobile site is significantly slower than desktop
High Impact

Your mobile score is 66 — that's 25 points below desktop. When someone searches for restaurants on their phone while standing on the sidewalk, your site loads noticeably slower than competitors. 53% of mobile visitors abandon after 3 seconds. Every second counts when a hungry customer has five other options one Google tap away.

🔍
Google has no description for your listing
High Impact

When someone searches "restaurants near me" on their phone, Google shows your restaurant name — but no meta description beneath it. Your competitors show "Reserve a table — daily specials available" or "Open now — authentic Asian cuisine." Without that hook, customers tap their listing instead of yours. You're invisible in the exact moment a hungry person is deciding where to eat.

⚡
Wix platform bloat is slowing down menu visibility
Medium Impact

Your site is built on Wix, which loads dozens of polyfills, fallback bundles, and platform code before your actual menu appears. The HTML shows multiple legacy polyfills, segmenter scripts, and essential viewer models that don't benefit your customers — they just add weight and delay. On cellular data, this bloat means your hours and menu take longer to load than they should.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

✍️
Write a meta description that converts phone searches

Add a 155-character meta description that tells Google what to show when someone searches "restaurants near me" in your area. Include your cuisine type, a key benefit (reservations, delivery, authentic), and call-to-action. This is the difference between a click and a swipe past.

Expected impact
↑ Google shows your description — more clicks from search
📲
Migrate away from Wix to a mobile-first platform

Wix's architecture loads unnecessary polyfills and platform code that slows mobile performance. Moving to a modern restaurant platform (like Toast, Square, or a React-based custom build) removes 50+ KB of unused JavaScript and brings your mobile score up to match desktop. Your menu, hours, and reservation button will load 2–3 seconds faster on phones.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
🎯
Optimize for FIFA 2026 local search traffic

Thousands of tourists are in your area for FIFA 2026 searching "restaurants near me" on their phones right now. With a faster mobile site and a compelling meta description, you can capture walk-in traffic from out-of-towners before they find your competitor. Speed + description = visibility + walk-ins during the biggest sports event year.

Expected impact
↑ FIFA fans find you first in local search
