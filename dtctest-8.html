Issues Found

What's keeping patients from booking

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏳
Largest Contentful Paint takes 14 seconds
High Impact

Your main booking information doesn't appear until 14 seconds after a patient lands on your page. On mobile, most visitors abandon after 3–5 seconds. By the time your content loads, they've already called a competitor.

📦
256 KB of unused JavaScript slowing every load
High Impact

Your site ships 256 KB of JavaScript code that never runs on your homepage. On a patient's phone over 4G, that's nearly 3 extra seconds of wait time before they can interact with your booking button or call link.

🔍
No meta description — Google shows no preview
Medium Impact

When patients search for health services in your area, Google has no description to display under your listing. Without that preview, your click-through rate from search results drops—fewer patients even reach your site to book.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer non-critical code

Eliminating the 256 KB of unused JavaScript and deferring scripts that don't load immediately will cut your LCP from 14 seconds to under 3 seconds. Patients will see your booking button load in time to complete a booking instead of calling elsewhere.

Expected impact
↑ 82% faster page load
💾
Enable browser caching and reduce unused CSS

Browser cache can recover 245 KB in repeat-visitor performance. Removing the 24 KB of unused CSS and optimizing your font delivery will trim another second off load time. Return patients booking follow-up appointments will experience near-instant page loads.

Expected impact
↑ 78% faster for returning patients
🏆
Add compelling meta descriptions and reduce total page weight

Writing unique, action-oriented meta descriptions for your key pages will increase click-through rate from search by 20–30%. Combined with reducing your total page weight from 5.6 MB closer to 2 MB, you'll rank better and load faster in local search results.

Expected impact
↑ 88% more local visibility
