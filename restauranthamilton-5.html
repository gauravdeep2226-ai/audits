Issues Found

Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Performance metrics unavailable — can't measure if your menu loads fast enough
High Impact

Your site's speed data isn't being captured — we can't see how long your menu, hours, or reservation button take to appear on a customer's phone. Without visibility into load times, you're flying blind. A hungry person on their phone needs your menu in under 3 seconds or they scroll to the next restaurant in Google. You won't know if you're losing them.

🔍
No meta description — Google has nothing to show when someone searches "restaurants near me"
High Impact

When a potential customer searches "restaurants near me" on Google, your listing appears — but with no description underneath. Your competitor's listing shows "Award-winning Italian — Reserve online — Open till 11pm." They get the tap. You get scrolled past. A meta description is a 160-character sales pitch that runs every single day. You're not using it.

📱
Wix platform bloat — your site loads heavy code that slows down mobile customers
Medium Impact

Your Wix site is loading multiple large bundle scripts (handleAccessTokens, overrideGlobals, polyfills) in the head section before the page renders. On cellular data, this overhead delays when a customer sees your menu, hours, or phone number. Every extra second a hungry person waits is a second closer to them tapping the restaurant below you in search results.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

📊
Enable and monitor Core Web Vitals tracking

Set up Google Analytics and Search Console to capture real load-time data from actual customers visiting your site. Once you're measuring, you'll know exactly how fast (or slow) your menu loads on phones, which lets you act on the data that matters most: are walk-in customers seeing your hours before they leave the page?

Expected impact
↑ See exactly how fast customers experience your site
✍️
Add a high-impact meta description

Write a 160-character description that includes your cuisine type, reservation availability, hours, and a hook: "Seasoned Restaurant — Fresh seasonal menu — Reserve online — Open daily. Award-winning dining in downtown [City]." This description shows up in every Google search result and tells customers why they should click you instead of your competitor.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me"
⚡
Migrate to a performance-first platform or optimize Wix rendering

Wix's architecture loads heavy overhead by default. Either migrate key pages to a lighter platform (like Next.js or Astro) or work with Wix support to defer non-critical scripts and use lazy loading for images. Every 500ms you cut off load time brings more walk-in customers from "restaurants near me" before they leave your site.

Expected impact
↑ Menu and hours load in under 2.5 seconds
