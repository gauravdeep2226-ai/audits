What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems from your live site pulling away sales.

⏱️
Slow Product Page Load Blocks Checkout
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 4.1 seconds — visitors stare at a blank or incomplete page for over 4 seconds before product images and pricing appear. On mobile checkout, every second of lag kills conversions; roughly 1 in 5 shoppers abandon before your Add to Cart button becomes interactive.

📦
229 KB of Unused JavaScript Slows Mobile Checkout
High Impact

Your site loads 229 KB of code that never runs, especially punishing on cellular connections. This dead weight delays the entire checkout flow, pushing load time further past the critical 3-second threshold where cart abandonment spikes.

🔍
Missing Meta Descriptions Kill Click-Through from Search
High Impact

Your product and content pages have no meta descriptions (0/1 on Google's scoring). When people search for pet allergy solutions, Google shows a blank preview instead of a compelling description of what you offer — meaning fewer clicks, fewer sales, even when you rank well.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact on your bottom line.

⚡
Remove Unused JavaScript and Defer Non-Critical Code

Strip the 229 KB of unused JavaScript and defer heavy third-party scripts. Defer Shopify analytics, captcha logic, and feature loaders until after the page is interactive. This unblocks rendering and keeps First Contentful Paint under 2 seconds on mobile.

Expected impact
↑ 55% faster checkout load
🖼️
Optimize Images and Implement Lazy Loading

Your total payload is 2.9 MB — likely driven by unoptimized product images. Convert to WebP, add responsive sizing, and lazy-load below-fold images. This cuts the time before product images and prices appear, so visitors see what you're selling instantly.

Expected impact
↑ 2.1s faster LCP
📝
Write Unique Meta Descriptions for Every Product

Add a 160-character meta description to every product page describing your solution for pet allergies, benefits, and unique value. This appears in Google search results, increases click-through by up to 30%, and ensures people actually arrive on your site instead of a competitor's.

Expected impact
↑ 20–30% organic traffic
