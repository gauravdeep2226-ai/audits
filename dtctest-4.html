Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Product pages take 55.7 seconds to fully load
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) is 55.7 seconds — visitors stare at a blank or incomplete page for nearly a minute before seeing your product images and Add to Cart button. In e-commerce, every second of delay drops conversions by 7%. At this speed, you're losing the majority of mobile shoppers before checkout even appears.

📦
1.1 MB of unused code blocks checkout flow
High Impact

Your site ships 591 KB of unused JavaScript and 74 KB of unused CSS — totaling over 665 KB of wasted bandwidth on every visit. On a 4G connection, that's 8+ extra seconds before a shopper can add dolls to their cart. Mobile users on cellular abandon at this delay rate.

🔍
Product images have no alt text for Google
Medium Impact

Your image alt text score is 0 — Google's crawler cannot read your doll product descriptions from images. This kills your visibility in Google Image Search and reduces SEO ranking for "American Girl dolls" queries. Customers searching for specific doll styles won't find you.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove unused code and lazy-load images

Audit and defer non-critical JavaScript. Implement lazy-loading for product images below the fold. Minify and tree-shake unused CSS. This alone recovers the 665 KB bottleneck and cuts the "wait for Add to Cart" time from 55+ seconds to under 3 seconds.

Expected impact
↑ 18x faster checkout
🖼️
Add alt text to all product images

Write descriptive alt text for every doll, playset, and accessory image (e.g., "Julie Albright 18-inch doll with red hair and vintage 1970s outfit"). Enable Google Image Search to index your products and improve keyword ranking for product-specific searches.

Expected impact
↑ Image search visibility
🔗
Ensure all links have descriptive anchor text

Your link text score is 0 — replace generic "Click here" or "Learn more" links with descriptive phrases like "Shop Julie Albright 18-inch doll" or "View playsets under $50." This improves both user clarity and SEO ranking for long-tail keywords.

Expected impact
↑ Better SEO ranking
