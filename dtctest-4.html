Issues Found

What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏳
Bloated JavaScript delays your storefront
High Impact

Your site loads 191 KiB of unused JavaScript — code that never runs on mobile. This bloat adds 1–2 seconds to your load time on cellular, delaying the moment shoppers see your products and hit Add to Cart. Every second of delay costs you conversions.

🖼️
Largest Contentful Paint takes 4.9 seconds
High Impact

Your main product images and content don't appear until nearly 5 seconds after a visitor lands on your site. On mobile over 4G, roughly 1 in 5 shoppers bounce before the page fully loads. This directly kills your conversion rate.

🎨
Unused CSS adds unnecessary page weight
Medium Impact

60 KiB of unused CSS sits on your page but never gets applied — wasted kilobytes that slow your initial render. Removing this would speed up First Contentful Paint by roughly 10–15%, letting customers see your brand faster.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚙️
Code-split and defer non-critical JavaScript

Identify the 191 KiB of unused JS (likely analytics, tracking, and third-party widgets) and load it only when needed — after the page is interactive. Shopify theme and secondary.js files should be audited for lazy-loading opportunities. This removes the main blocker to FCP.

Expected impact
↑ 2–3s faster initial load
📦
Optimize and compress product images

Your LCP assets are likely large product images. Serve them as WebP with aggressive compression, and use responsive images (srcset) to serve smaller versions on mobile. Consider lazy-loading below-the-fold product grids. A 4.9s LCP should drop to under 2.5s with proper image optimization.

Expected impact
↑ 40–50% faster LCP
🧹
Purge and optimize stylesheet

Audit the stylesheet for dead CSS rules (60 KiB is a significant amount). Tools like PurgeCSS can strip unused selectors. Shrine theme pro may have default styles for components you don't use — remove those imports or use a more minimal theme variant. This won't move the needle on conversions alone, but combined with the above, it shaves 200–300ms.

Expected impact
↑ 10–15% FCP improvement
