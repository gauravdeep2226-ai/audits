Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems slowing down your store and blocking checkout.

⏱️
Massive page load delay kills conversions
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 7.4 seconds on mobile — customers stare at a blank screen for nearly 8 seconds before products appear. On cellular, roughly 1 in 4 shoppers abandon before the Add to Cart button loads. Every second above 3s costs measurable revenue.

🖼️
Product images lack alt text, blocking SEO visibility
High Impact

Your SEO audit shows 0 on image alt text — Google can't understand what your products are. Customers searching "pet hair remover" or similar can't find you via image search. This directly reduces organic traffic and lost sales from search.

💾
4.3 MB of unused code slowing every visitor
Medium Impact

Your site carries 23 KiB of unused JavaScript and 18 KiB of unused CSS — code that never runs but every customer downloads. On a 4G phone, that's wasted seconds. Combined with slow LCP, this delays checkout and increases mobile cart abandonment.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Optimize and lazy-load product images

Compress product images to WebP format, implement lazy-loading for below-fold images, and preload hero images. Remove render-blocking resources. This will drop LCP from 7.4s to under 3s and let the Add to Cart button appear before customers leave.

Expected impact
↑ 57% faster load, ↑ conversions
🔍
Add descriptive alt text to all product images

Write unique, keyword-rich alt text for every product photo (e.g., "pet hair remover glove for furniture and clothes"). This lets Google index your images, opens image search traffic, and improves product discoverability from organic search.

Expected impact
↑ Image search traffic
🧹
Remove unused JavaScript and CSS bundles

Audit and remove 23 KiB of unused JS and 18 KiB of unused CSS. Tree-shake your JavaScript bundles and defer non-critical theme scripts. This reduces total payload and speeds up JavaScript parsing on slower mobile connections.

Expected impact
↑ 12% faster initial load
