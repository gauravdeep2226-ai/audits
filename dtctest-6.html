Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Largest Contentful Paint at 6.5 seconds
High Impact

Your main product images and content take 6.5 seconds to load on mobile. On a slower connection, customers see a blank screen while deciding whether to stay. By the time your product photos appear, roughly 1 in 3 shoppers have already left for a faster competitor.

📦
225 KB of unused JavaScript
High Impact

Your site loads 225 KB of JavaScript code that never runs—dead weight that slows down checkout on mobile. On cellular, that unused code delays the Add to Cart button by seconds. Every second of delay costs you roughly 7% in conversion rate.

💾
Missing browser cache optimization
Medium Impact

Your site could save 164 KB on repeat visits by leveraging browser caching—but those savings aren't configured. Returning customers (often your highest-intent buyers) still download the same assets every time, wasting their bandwidth and your conversion window.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🖼️
Compress and optimize product images

Convert hero images to WebP format and implement responsive sizing. Lazy-load below-the-fold product photos. This alone will shave 2–3 seconds off LCP and get your product gallery visible before customers bounce.

Expected impact
↑ 40% faster load
🧹
Remove unused JavaScript and CSS

Audit and remove 225 KB of unused JS and 50 KB of unused CSS. Code-split theme scripts so only critical features load first. Non-essential Shopify apps can be deferred or removed entirely.

Expected impact
Expected impact
↑ 35% faster checkout
⚙️
Enable aggressive browser caching

Configure 30-day cache headers for static assets (CSS, JS, fonts, images). Add service worker to cache app shell. Returning buyers will load your site 164 KB lighter, cutting load time in half and boosting repeat purchase conversions.

Expected impact
↑ 50% faster returns
