Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Rebuild your mobile experience to load under 3 seconds

Optimize images for mobile screens, defer non-critical JavaScript, and prioritize your menu and reservation button above the fold. Test on real 4G to ensure every hungry customer sees your offer within seconds. This directly addresses your 60 mobile score and the 53% abandonment rate.

Expected impact
↑ Mobile customers reach your menu before they leave
📝
Add a rich meta description and schema markup

Write a compelling meta description that includes your cuisine type, hours, and reservation link. Add Restaurant schema JSON-LD so Google displays your hours, ratings, and menu link directly in search results. This is what shows up when someone searches "restaurants near me" — make sure you're not invisible anymore.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me" and "dining in Hamilton"
🚀
Reduce font loading and render-blocking resources

Swap out the seven Google font families for a system font stack or a single optimized font. Use font-display: swap so text appears immediately while fonts load in the background. This removes the invisible delay that makes customers see a blank screen — they'll see your menu, hours, and phone number instantly.

Expected impact
↑ FIFA fans and walk-ins find you first on Google
