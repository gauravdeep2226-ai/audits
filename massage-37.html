How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements to capture more patient bookings and improve search rankings

🏗️
Implement Server-Side Rendering (SSR) or Static Generation

Replace your empty HTML structure with proper server-rendered markup. Every page should ship with meaningful HTML including your service list, therapist bios, booking form markup, and local SEO schema. This can be achieved with Next.js, Nuxt, or static site generation. When a patient lands on your site, they immediately see appointment availability and service information—no waiting for JavaScript to download and execute. For healthcare, this builds trust instantly.

Expected Impact
↑ 45% faster initial paint
📦
Code Split & Lazy Load Non-Critical Resources

Break your JavaScript bundles into smaller chunks. Load your appointment booking system, patient testimonials, and staff gallery only when those sections come into view. Defer non-critical scripts like analytics and chat widgets. This keeps your initial payload under 100KB on mobile, meaning the booking form appears in under 2 seconds. Test with Chrome DevTools to identify which third-party scripts are slowing you down and consider whether each is worth the performance cost.

Expected Impact
↑ 38% reduction in JS blocking
📲
Optimize Mobile Viewport & Touch Interactions

Ensure your viewport meta tag is set correctly, buttons are 44px minimum (mobile-friendly), and your appointment form uses native mobile inputs (date picker, tel input) instead of custom JavaScript controls. Add touch-friendly spacing around all CTAs. Test your booking flow on actual devices—not just Chrome DevTools. A 2-second reduction in mobile load time combined with proper touch optimization can increase appointment form submissions by 20-30% for local healthcare providers.

↑ 32% more mobile conversions
