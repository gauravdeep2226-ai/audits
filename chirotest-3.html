Issues Found

What's costing you chiropractic bookings

Three measured problems preventing patients from scheduling appointments.

⏱️
Critical images load too slowly
High Impact

Your largest images take 9.9 seconds to appear on mobile. A patient searching "chiropractor Thunder Bay" views your homepage, sees a blank screen for nearly 10 seconds, and calls a competitor instead. This alone is costing you bookings every single day.

👁️
Images missing accessibility labels
High Impact

Your images lack alt text (SEO score: 0 for image alt). Google can't tell what your photos show—whether they're your clinic interior, your chiropractor, or your treatment room. This directly hurts your "near me" local ranking because Google has less confidence in your content relevance.

📦
Unused code drags down load speed
Medium Impact

Your site ships 281 KB of JavaScript that never runs, and 124 KB of unused CSS. On cellular, that's extra seconds patients wait before they can see your booking button or click to call. Combined, that's roughly 405 KB wasted per visit—nearly 25% of your total page weight.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🖼️
Optimize and lazy-load images

Compress your images to WebP format, serve responsive sizes for mobile, and lazy-load below-the-fold photos. This cuts image size by 60–70% and ensures your hero image and service photos load in under 2 seconds on 4G, so patients see your clinic immediately and book.

Expected impact
↓ LCP from 9.9s to ~2.5s
🏷️
Add descriptive alt text to all images

Write unique alt text for every image: clinic photos, practitioner headshots, treatment room, equipment. Google will immediately understand your content, rank you higher for "chiropractor Thunder Bay," and improve your local SEO visibility for nearby patients actively searching for care.

Expected impact
↑ Local ranking, ↑ Image alt score to 1
🧹
Remove unused JavaScript and CSS

Audit and remove the 281 KB of unused JavaScript and 124 KB of unused CSS (likely from theme bloat or unused plugins). Keep only the code needed for booking flows, contact forms, and essential navigation. This saves 405 KB per patient, cutting page bloat by a quarter.

Expected impact
↑ ~20% faster overall load
