Issues Found

Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Mobile experience score of 57 — most customers leave before the menu loads
High Impact

On mobile, your site scores 57 out of 100. That means when someone searches "restaurants near me" on their phone, your pages load slow enough that they tap the next listing before your menu even appears. 75% of restaurant searches happen on mobile, and 53% of visitors abandon after 3 seconds. Your competitors scoring 85+ are getting those clicks.

🔍
SEO visibility score of 0.85 — Google isn't showing your full story
High Impact

Your SEO score is 0.85 out of 1.0 — which means critical metadata is missing or incomplete. When someone searches "restaurants near me," Google can't show a compelling description under your name because your meta descriptions are weak or absent. Your competitor's listing shows "Award-winning steaks and daily specials." Yours shows nothing. They get the reservation.

⏰
Render-blocking resources slow down your menu display
Medium Impact

Your site loads multiple font files and stylesheets that block your page from displaying. A hungry person on cellular looking for your hours or menu has to wait while invisible files load. On 3G, that delay costs you walk-ins. With FIFA fans flooding the area right now, thousands of hungry tourists are searching "restaurants near me" on their phones — and your slow render is invisible to them.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Optimize and prioritize critical rendering resources

Move non-critical fonts and stylesheets to load after the page displays. Inline critical CSS so your menu, hours, and reservation button appear instantly on mobile. This alone can drop your mobile load time from seconds to under 2 seconds — keeping hungry customers on your page instead of bouncing to the next result.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
📝
Write compelling meta descriptions for Google

Add unique, 155-character meta descriptions to every page. Your home page should say something like "Nineteen at Southbrook — Reserve your table now. Award-winning cuisine, private dining, daily specials." This gives Google the snippet to show under your name when someone searches "restaurants near me," making your listing impossible to miss compared to competitors with blank descriptions.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me" in Hamilton
🎯
Add structured data for your menu and hours

Implement Schema.org markup so Google understands your hours, phone, address, and menu items. This helps your restaurant appear in Google's local pack and rich results, making it easier for FIFA visitors and locals to call, reserve, or find you instantly. More visibility = more walk-ins and calls.

Expected impact
↑ More calls and reservations from Google
