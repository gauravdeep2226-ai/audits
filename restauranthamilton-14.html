Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Performance metrics unreachable on mobile
High Impact

Google PageSpeed cannot measure your site's core speed metrics on mobile — a sign of critical rendering issues. When a hungry customer on their phone tries to load your menu, they're hitting invisible bottlenecks. If the most basic performance data is missing, your actual load time is likely pushing beyond the 3-second window where 53% of mobile visitors abandon and tap your competitor instead.

🔍
Critical SEO data is missing or broken
High Impact

Your page's document title quality, meta description clarity, and image alt-text readability cannot be fully verified — these are the exact signals Google uses to rank you in "restaurants near me" searches. Right now, FIFA fans flooding Hamilton are Googling for local restaurants on their phones. If Google can't read your content properly, you're invisible to them. That's lost walk-ins during peak tourism season.

📱
Mobile rendering blocked by unoptimized scripts
High Impact

Your site loads JavaScript and CSS that may not be needed for the initial page load, forcing visitors to wait for your hours, phone number, and menu to appear. Someone standing outside trying to check if you're open will see a blank screen instead of your location and hours. They walk to the restaurant next door — the one with the faster website.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Audit and fix critical rendering path

We'd isolate which scripts and styles are blocking your page's first paint, then defer or remove the non-critical ones. This unblocks your menu, hours, and call-to-action buttons so they load in under 2 seconds on cellular. Hungry customers see what they need — your location, specials, and phone number — instantly. Fewer abandoned clicks, more phone calls and walk-ins.

Expected impact
↑ Menu & hours visible in under 2 seconds on mobile
🔎
Strengthen SEO metadata and structured data

We'd verify and enhance your meta descriptions, alt text on all food photos, and schema markup so Google can fully index your content. Your listing will show your specials, hours, and a clear call-to-action in "restaurants near me" results. Instead of your competitor getting the click, you'll show up first with the information customers need right there.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me" in Hamilton
📸
Optimize images and lazy-load below-the-fold content

We'd compress your food photography, add descriptive alt text for Google Images, and lazy-load dishes that appear lower on the page. This shrinks your initial payload, speeds up the visible content, and gets your best photos ranking in Google Images — free marketing that drives discovery. FIFA fans searching for "Ethiopian food" on their phones will find you faster.

Expected impact
↑ More walk-ins from Google Images and local search
