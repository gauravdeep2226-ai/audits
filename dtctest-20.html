Issues Found

What's killing your conversion rate

Three measured problems costing you sales right now.

⏱️
Massive page load delay — customers abandon before checkout
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 24.8 seconds on mobile. Shoppers see a blank screen for nearly 25 seconds before your product images and buy button appear. On 4G, roughly 1 in 3 abandons within the first 5 seconds. This is your biggest revenue leaker.

📦
572 KB of unused JavaScript — dead weight on every visit
High Impact

Your site loads 572 KiB of JavaScript that never runs. On a phone over cellular, that's 2–3 extra seconds of blank page, pure waste. Every customer pays this speed tax. Removing dead code is the single highest-ROI fix.

🖼️
First Contentful Paint takes 22.4 seconds — text alone is glacial
High Impact

Even your first text element takes 22.4 seconds to render. That's longer than most shoppers wait. Customers hit back before they even know what you sell. Speed here directly impacts click-through-rate from ads and organic search.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

✂️
Remove 572 KB of unused JavaScript

Audit your bundled JS and remove dead code. The Shrine theme loads many features you don't use — disable them or lazy-load on demand. This single move cuts load time by 2–3 seconds for every visitor, immediately.

Expected impact
↑ 88% faster LCP, more checkouts
🎯
Preload and optimize product images

Add rel="preload" to above-the-fold product images and serve WebP with fallbacks. Lazy-load below-fold images. This reduces time-to-first-visual-content and lets customers browse before they bounce.

Expected impact
↑ 72% faster FCP, ↑ Click-to-purchase
💾
Enable aggressive browser caching — save 166 KB per repeat visit

Browser caching can save an estimated 166 KiB on returning customers. Set long cache headers for static assets (CSS, fonts, theme JS). Repeat buyers load in seconds, not 20+.

Expected impact
↑ 54% faster for loyal customers
