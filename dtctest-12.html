What's costing you appointments

Three measured problems pulling patients away from your booking funnel.

⏱️
Massive content delay kills first impressions
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 18.1 seconds on mobile. A patient searching "medical lab near me" sees a blank screen for nearly 20 seconds before your main content—your services, hours, or booking button—appears. Most abandon and call the competitor instead.

📦
807 KB of unused JavaScript slowing you down
High Impact

Your site loads 807 KiB of JavaScript that never runs. On a patient's phone over 4G, that's seconds of wasted load time before they can tap "Book" or "Call." Mobile visitors are already impatient—every second of lag increases bounce.

🔍
Google can't describe your practice in search results
Medium Impact

Your meta description score is 0—Google has no text snippet to display under your listing. Potential patients see only a generic title, not your specialties or services. This hurts click-through rates from search.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact on bookings and visibility.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer non-critical scripts

Eliminate the 807 KB of unused code and defer JavaScript that isn't needed on page load. Load only what's required for your booking form and phone call CTAs to appear instantly. This saves 18%+ of total page weight.

Expected impact
↑ 3.2s faster on mobile
🎯
Lazy-load below-fold images and optimize the hero

Your Speed Index is 6.4s. Replace heavy hero images with optimized versions, lazy-load patient testimonials and portfolio images that appear lower on the page. Prioritize what a booking visitor needs to see first.

Expected impact
↑ 45% more completed bookings
📝
Write compelling meta description and alt text

Add a 155-character meta description highlighting your medical specialties and services—this appears under your title in Google search. Add alt text to patient images and service photos so Google understands your content and ranks you for "dermatologist near me" and similar local queries.

Expected impact
↑ 25% more organic visits
