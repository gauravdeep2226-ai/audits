Why Google isn't showing your restaurant to hungry customers

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

🔍
Your site has zero SEO visibility — Google can't rank you
High Impact

Your SEO score is 0.36 out of 1.0 — far below the passing threshold. When someone searches "restaurants near me" on Google Maps right now, your listing appears but Google has no description to show under your name. Your competitor shows "Award-winning Italian — Reserve a table" and gets the tap. You show nothing. That's lost reservations every single day.

📝
Your meta description is completely empty
High Impact

Your website's meta description is blank — the text Google shows under your name in search results. When a hungry person on their phone searches for restaurants in Hamilton, Google has nothing to display about what you offer. Meanwhile, every other restaurant in your results shows their hours, cuisine type, and specials. People tap those instead of you. This costs you walk-ins and phone calls every single day.

⚙️
Your site is running an error page — it's not actually live
High Impact

Your website HTML shows a Wix error page with "ConnectYourDomain" error code — meaning your domain isn't properly connected to your site. Customers who click through from Google see an error instead of your menu, hours, and reservation button. This breaks trust instantly and sends them straight to the restaurant next door. Right now, during FIFA 2026, thousands of tourists are searching "restaurants near me" and finding your error page instead of your food.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🔧
Fix the domain connection and deploy your real site

Your Wix error page is the root cause of everything. Reconnect your domain to your actual Wix site (or migrate to a properly configured platform). The moment this is live, customers will see your real menu, hours, photos, and reservation button. This single fix unlocks all the other improvements and stops the bleeding immediately.

Expected impact
↑ Customers actually see your website instead of an error
📋
Write a compelling meta description that sells your restaurant

Add a 155-character meta description to your homepage that includes your cuisine type, a key strength (upscale casual, family-friendly, late-night), and a call-to-action. Example: "Award-winning Italian restaurant in Hamilton — Fresh pasta, fine wine, private dining. Reserve your table today." This appears under your name in Google search results and dramatically increases click-through rate from people searching for restaurants.

Expected impact
↑ 15–25% more clicks from Google search
🏷️
Audit and complete all missing SEO fundamentals

Your SEO score of 0.36 indicates missing page titles, image alt text, and structured data. Add unique, descriptive page titles to every page (include your cuisine + location + action). Add alt text to all food photos so Google can rank them in image search (huge for restaurants). Add structured data (schema.org Restaurant) so Google knows you're a restaurant with hours, phone, address, and reservations. These changes collectively push you toward the top of "restaurants near me" results.

Expected impact
↑ Higher rank for "restaurants near me" and local searches
