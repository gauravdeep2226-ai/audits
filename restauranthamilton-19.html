Issues Found

Why hungry customers are picking the diner next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Mobile site crawls on cellular — customers abandon before your menu appears
High Impact

Your mobile score of 44 means the site is painfully slow on phones. When someone stands outside your restaurant on cellular data, Joy's Cafe menu takes too long to load. 53% of mobile visitors leave after 3 seconds — by the time your hours and phone number appear, they're already Googling the truck stop down the road.

🔍
Google can't see most of your images — you're invisible in local search results
High Impact

With FIFA fans flooding Hamilton right now, thousands are searching "restaurants near me" on their phones. Your images lack alt text — so Google can't read what you're serving. This costs you visibility in Google Images and Local results. Your competitor with properly labeled photos shows up first, and you lose the walk-in.

⚡
Render-blocking resources delay your menu by seconds — browsers wait, customers don't
High Impact

Your site loads render-blocking CSS and JavaScript before showing the menu or reservation button. A hungry customer on a slow connection sees a blank screen instead of your specials. Every second of delay is a customer calling ahead to Perkins instead.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Defer non-critical CSS and async-load JavaScript

Move render-blocking resources below the fold and load them asynchronously. This surfaces your menu, hours, and Joy's Cafe location within 1.5 seconds on mobile — fast enough that a hungry customer on their phone actually sees what you serve.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads in under 2 seconds on cellular
🖼️
Add descriptive alt text to all food and location images

Write alt text for your truck stop photos, Joy's Cafe plating, and facility shots. This lets Google index and rank your images in local search. When someone searches "restaurants near me," your food photos show up — and they click through to your site instead of a competitor.

Expected impact
↑ Visible in Google Images and local search
📊
Optimize images and remove unused CSS to cut mobile payload

Compress your slider images (currently large), minify CSS, and remove unused stylesheets. This shrinks your initial load on cellular data. Faster site = more walk-ins from "restaurants near me" searches and more reservation calls during peak hours.

Expected impact
↑ 30% faster on mobile — more customer conversions
