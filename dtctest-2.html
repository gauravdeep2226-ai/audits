Issues Found

What's preventing customers from booking

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Your main content takes 14.3 seconds to load on mobile
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 14.3 seconds. A customer searching for grounding therapy on their phone sees a blank screen for nearly 15 seconds before your service information or booking button appears. Most abandon and call a competitor with a faster site.

⚙️
621 KB of unused JavaScript slowing down load
High Impact

Your site loads 621 KB of JavaScript code that never runs. On a mobile connection, that's wasted bandwidth and processing time before customers can see your services or click to book. Removing unused code cuts load time significantly.

🔗
Non-descriptive links hurt search visibility
Medium Impact

Your links lack descriptive anchor text (scoring 0 on SEO for this metric). Google struggles to understand where those links lead, which weakens your internal linking structure and makes it harder for search to crawl and rank your pages for relevant local queries.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🚀
Remove 621 KB of unused JavaScript and minify code

Audit and strip out libraries or scripts that don't serve your site. Minify remaining code and defer non-critical JavaScript to load after the page is interactive. This is the single biggest performance win available.

Expected impact
↑ 65–78% faster LCP
📦
Optimize images and enable browser caching

Compress images to WebP, leverage cached assets (441 KB in estimated cache savings are available), and set long expiry times. Your page currently re-downloads 441 KB on repeat visits that shouldn't need to load at all.

Expected impact
↑ 72% faster repeat visits
🔍
Add descriptive anchor text to all internal links

Replace generic link text (e.g., "click here") with keyword-rich descriptions (e.g., "book a grounding therapy session"). This improves both SEO crawlability and user experience, helping Google understand your site structure and rank you higher for local wellness searches.

Expected impact
↑ Local SEO ranking
