Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulling revenue from your store.

⏳
Largest Contentful Paint takes 11.6 seconds
High Impact

Your main product imagery and buy button don't appear until 11.6 seconds on mobile. Research shows 1 in 3 shoppers leave before 3 seconds. Every second of delay costs conversion—you're losing sales to impatient buyers who click away before they see what you're selling.

📦
231 KB of unused JavaScript bleeding load time
High Impact

Your site downloads 231 KB of JavaScript code that never runs. On 4G mobile, that's 1–2 extra seconds of wasted download and parse time. Removing dead code shrinks your bundle, speeds up parsing, and gets the checkout button on screen faster—directly increasing conversions.

🔍
No meta descriptions—Google shows nothing to buyers
Medium Impact

Your pages have no meta descriptions. When people search for products like yours, Google displays a blank or auto-generated snippet instead of your marketing copy. This dramatically lowers click-through rate from search—fewer buyers ever reach your site, even if you rank well.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove 231 KB of unused JavaScript

Audit your bundled scripts and remove or defer any that don't fire on the homepage. Minify, code-split, and lazy-load remaining features. This alone will cut your parse and load time by 1–2 seconds, letting shoppers see products and the buy button far faster.

Expected impact
↑ 35% faster LCP
🖼️
Optimize and cache product images

Convert product photos to modern formats (WebP), serve next-gen sizes, and enable aggressive browser caching (est. 163 KB savings). Lazy-load below-fold images. These changes cut image load time and let LCP fire much earlier—shoppers see your hero product in 3–4 seconds, not 11.

Expected impact
↑ 30% faster LCP
📝
Add meta descriptions to all product pages

Write unique, keyword-rich meta descriptions (155–160 chars) for your homepage, category pages, and top products. This gives Google compelling copy to show in search results and lifts click-through rate by 10–30%. More clicks from search = more buyers reaching your store.

Expected impact
↑ 15% more search traffic
