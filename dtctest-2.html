Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Massive initial page load — 8.5 seconds to show content
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 8.5 seconds on mobile. E-commerce research shows roughly 1 in 3 shoppers abandon a site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Customers searching for shoes on mobile are hitting back before your product images even appear.

🚫
1.7 MB of unused JavaScript blocking checkout speed
High Impact

Your site loads 1,723 KB of JavaScript that never runs on the homepage. This bloats the initial load and delays the Add to Cart interaction. On cellular, this unused code translates directly to lost conversions — every second of delay costs revenue.

📄
Search engines can't describe your site to shoppers
Medium Impact

Your meta description is missing (0/1 scoring element). Google has no description to show under your listing in search results, so fewer people click through. Shoppers searching for shoes see a blank snippet instead of compelling copy — you're invisible against competitors with descriptions.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer below-the-fold assets

The 1.7 MB of unused code can be code-split and lazy-loaded, and non-critical dependencies can be deferred. Combined with aggressive caching of the remaining 6.4 MB, this cuts your Time to Interactive from 2,050ms to under 600ms, letting shoppers interact with products much faster.

Expected impact
↑ 4+ seconds faster checkout
🖼️
Optimize image delivery with WebP and responsive sizing

Product images on shoe sites are often the largest files. Serve modern formats (WebP) to mobile, lazy-load below-the-fold images, and use srcset for responsive variants. This alone can shave 2–3 seconds off LCP, moving your site from 8.5s to under 5.5s on cellular.

Expected impact
Expected impact
↑ 35% faster page paint
🔍
Add meta description and optimize title tags

Write a compelling 155–160 character meta description for each collection and product category. Ensure title tags include brand + primary keyword (e.g. "Women's Running Shoes | Fast Shipping – Shoebacca"). This improves click-through rate from Google and signals relevance to the algorithm.

Expected impact
↑ 15–25% more organic traffic
