What's slowing down customer conversions

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Critical delay before product content loads
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 12.6 seconds on mobile. Shoppers see a blank screen for over 12 seconds before images and product details appear. Most online buyers abandon within 3 seconds — you're losing sales before anything loads.

📦
163 KB of unused JavaScript slowing checkout
High Impact

Your pages load 163 KB of JavaScript code that never executes. On cellular connections, this alone adds 2–3 seconds to page load time. Every second delay in checkout flow costs revenue.

🔍
Missing product descriptions in search results
Medium Impact

Your meta descriptions are not present (0/1 on SEO audit). When customers search Google, they see no preview text — just your URL. Competitors with descriptions get more clicks. This directly impacts organic traffic and discoverability.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer non-critical scripts

Strip the 163 KB of unused JS and defer module scripts that load after the main product image. Combined with browser caching strategies (254 KB available savings), we can cut LCP from 12.6s to under 3 seconds on mobile.

Expected impact
↑ 75% faster load
🖼️
Optimize and lazy-load product images

Your total page weight is 2.6 MB — images are likely the bulk. Compress product photos to WebP format, lazy-load below-the-fold images, and defer rendering until after the first product hero loads. This directly speeds up FCP (currently 5.7s).

Expected impact
Expected impact
↑ More immediate conversions
📝
Write unique meta descriptions for all products

Add 150–160 character meta descriptions to every product page. Google will display these in search results, encouraging more clicks from organic traffic. Pair with product schema markup to enable rich snippets.

Expected impact
↑ 15–30% more search clicks
