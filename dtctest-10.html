Issues Found

What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏳
Largest Contentful Paint takes 12.6 seconds
High Impact

Your product images and main content don't appear until 12.6 seconds on mobile. In e-commerce, 1 in 4 shoppers abandon within the first 3 seconds. This delay alone is costing you meaningful cart completions before visitors even see what you're selling.

📦
116 KiB of unused JavaScript on every page load
High Impact

Your site ships 116 kilobytes of code that never runs. On a mobile connection, that's 2–3 extra seconds wasted before the page becomes interactive. Combined with your LCP delay, this creates a slow, frustrating experience that kills conversion rate.

🔎
No meta description in search results
Medium Impact

Google has no description to display under your listing in search results—only your page title appears. A blank snippet means fewer clicks. Better meta descriptions improve CTR by 5–15%, which is free traffic you're leaving on the table.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🖼️
Optimize product image delivery and lazy-load below-fold content

Compress hero images to WebP format, serve responsive sizes per device, and implement native lazy-loading for images below the fold. Pair this with a lightweight image CDN. This directly addresses your 12.6s LCP and 6.4s Speed Index.

Expected impact
↓ LCP to ~4s, ↑ Conversion by 8–12%
⚙️
Remove or defer unused JavaScript bundles

Audit your 116 KiB of unused JS (likely form handlers, tracking, or legacy code). Defer non-critical scripts and remove dead code. This eliminates parsing and execution delays that block interactivity on slower networks.

Expected impact
↓ Bundle by 35%, ↑ Interactive by ~2s
📝
Write unique meta descriptions for every page

Add a 150–160 character meta description that includes your primary keyword and a clear benefit (e.g., "Discover top-rated Korean skincare products reviewed by experts. Best serums, moisturizers & more"). This appears directly in Google search results and boosts click-through rate.

Expected impact
↑ CTR by 5–15%, ↑ Organic traffic
