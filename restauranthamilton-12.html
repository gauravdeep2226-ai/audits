Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Site is hosted on a design platform, not a real restaurant website
High Impact

Your site is currently a Canva design preview link showing an "unsupported browser" message. When someone finds you on Google and clicks through, they see an error page — not your menu, hours, or reservation option. A hungry customer on their phone doesn't wait for error screens; they tap the restaurant below you in the search results.

🔍
No SEO foundation — Google can't see or rank your restaurant
High Impact

Your SEO score is 0.66 — well below the 0.9+ threshold needed to compete. There's no proper restaurant name in the page title, no meta description for "restaurants near me" searches, and no way for Google to understand what you serve or where you're located. Right now, thousands of FIFA fans are searching for dining options on their phones — and your restaurant is invisible to them.

⏱️
Mobile performance bottleneck — site doesn't function on phones
High Impact

With a mobile score of 30, your site fails on the devices where 80%+ of restaurant searches happen. Customers standing on the sidewalk trying to check your hours, see your menu, or find your phone number are hitting a broken experience instead. That's walk-ins and reservation calls you're losing in real time.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🏗️
Move to a real restaurant website platform

Replace the Canva design preview with a proper restaurant website (WordPress, Webflow, or a dedicated restaurant platform). This gives you a working site with your menu, hours, phone number, reservation button, and all the essentials. No more error pages — just a clean, fast experience that Google can crawl and customers can actually use.

Expected impact
↑ Customers see your menu instead of an error page
🎯
Build SEO foundation so Google ranks you for local searches

Add a proper page title (e.g., "MacTini Bar — Best Cocktails & Food in Hamilton"), a meta description (e.g., "Craft cocktails, full menu, and reservations available. Open daily. Reserve your table today."), and local business schema markup. This tells Google exactly what you are, where you are, and why customers should choose you. With FIFA fans searching "restaurants near me" in Hamilton right now, this is free visibility.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me" in your area
⚡
Optimize for mobile-first performance

Use a fast, mobile-optimized hosting and framework. Lazy-load images, minimize render-blocking resources, and ensure your menu loads in under 2 seconds on a 4G connection. When someone pulls up your site while standing outside, they see your hours and menu instantly — not a spinning wheel. That's the difference between a walk-in and a missed customer.

Expected impact
↑ Walk-ins from "restaurants near me" searches
