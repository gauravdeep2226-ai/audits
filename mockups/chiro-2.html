FAQ

Common Questions

We know you may have questions before your first visit. Here are answers to the most common things our patients ask.

Your first visit includes a detailed health history review, a thorough orthopedic and neurological examination, and a discussion of your symptoms and goals. If X-rays are needed, we'll take them on-site. We'll then review all findings with you and present a customized care plan before any treatment begins — so you're always fully informed and comfortable.

The sound you hear during a chiropractic adjustment is called cavitation — it's simply the release of gas (nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide) from the fluid within a joint as it is gently mobilized back into proper position. It is completely harmless and is not bones grinding or cracking. Many patients actually find the sound satisfying and report immediate relief afterward.

The number of visits depends on the nature and severity of your condition, how long you've had it, and your personal health goals. Acute pain may resolve in as few as 6–10 visits, while chronic conditions or corrective care programs may require a longer commitment. We'll give you an honest, clear timeline at your initial assessment — and we never recommend more care than you actually need.

Yes — most major extended health insurance plans in Canada cover chiropractic care. Providers such as Blue Cross, Manulife, Sun Life, Canada Life, and GreenShield typically include chiropractic as a covered benefit. We recommend checking your specific plan's annual maximum. We can provide official receipts for direct submission or reimbursement. Coverage may also apply under MVA or Workers' Compensation claims.

Chiropractic care is one of the safest forms of healthcare for musculoskeletal conditions when performed by a licensed Doctor of Chiropractic. It is safe for infants, children, pregnant women, seniors, and athletes. Our chiropractors are fully trained to identify contraindications and modify techniques appropriately — ensuring every patient receives the safest and most effective care possible for their unique situation.
