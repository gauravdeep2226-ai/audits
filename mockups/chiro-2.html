About Us

Rooted in St. John's, Dedicated to Your Health

At Discover Chiropractic, we believe that optimal health begins with a well-functioning spine and nervous system. Our clinic was founded on the principle that every patient deserves individualized, compassionate care — not a one-size-fits-all approach. We take the time to truly understand your health history, lifestyle, and goals before crafting a care plan that works for you.

Serving the St. John's community from our clinic at 146 Campbell Ave, our Doctor of Chiropractic brings a thorough, evidence-informed approach to spinal health. We're proud of our 5-star reputation and the countless patients who have reclaimed their quality of life through chiropractic care.
