About Us

Rooted in Galway, Committed to Your Recovery

ProActive Wellness Centre has been a trusted chiropractic provider in the Galway neighbourhood of St. John's, Newfoundland. Our philosophy is simple: identify the root cause of your discomfort, not just the symptoms. We combine evidence-based chiropractic techniques with individualized care plans to deliver lasting results for every patient who walks through our doors.

Whether you're a desk worker battling chronic neck pain, an athlete recovering from an injury, a parent-to-be seeking pregnancy relief, or a child in need of gentle spinal care, our team is here to guide you through every step of your wellness journey. We are proud to serve the growing Galway community and surrounding areas of St. John's with the highest standard of chiropractic care.

  • Licensed Doctors of Chiropractic (DC) with advanced clinical training
  • Evidence-based, personalized treatment plans for every patient
  • Direct insurance billing for most major plans
  • Family-friendly clinic welcoming patients of all ages
  • Conveniently located in the growing Galway community, St. John's NL
