About Us

St. John's Most Trusted Chiropractic Team

At ProActive Wellness Centre, we believe that pain-free living should be accessible to everyone. Our registered chiropractors bring years of advanced clinical training and a genuine passion for patient outcomes — combining the latest evidence-based techniques with a thorough, individualized approach to care.

Located in the heart of St. John's, NL, we've helped thousands of patients recover from acute injuries, manage chronic conditions, and reach peak physical performance. Whether you're dealing with a recent flare-up or years of accumulated discomfort, our team is here to get you moving again.

  • Individualized treatment plans — no cookie-cutter approaches
  • Advanced soft tissue, rehabilitation, and spinal decompression techniques
  • Welcoming patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors
