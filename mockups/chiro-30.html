About Us

Chiropractic Care Built Around You

At In Motion Health Centre in St. John's, NL, we believe that optimal health is achieved through movement, alignment, and function. Our licensed chiropractors take a whole-body approach—addressing not just your symptoms, but the root cause of your discomfort so you can stay active and pain-free for the long term.

We combine evidence-based chiropractic techniques with patient education and individualized rehabilitation exercises. Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing a chronic condition, or simply looking to move better, our team is committed to helping you reach your health goals.

  • Licensed Doctors of Chiropractic (DC) – Regulated by the NL Chiropractic Board
  • Evidence-informed treatment plans personalized for each patient
  • Direct insurance billing for major providers
  • Serving St. John's and the surrounding Avalon Peninsula community
  • Comprehensive initial assessments with X-ray referral availability
