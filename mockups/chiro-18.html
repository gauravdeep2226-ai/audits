About Us

Rooted in Community. Focused on Your Health.

Back Home Chiropractic was founded on one simple belief: everyone deserves to live a life free from pain. Dr. Jeff Marshall brings a warm, patient-centered approach to chiropractic care, taking the time to truly understand each patient's history, lifestyle, and health goals before crafting a personalized treatment plan.

Serving the Goulds community and surrounding areas of Newfoundland, our clinic is built on trust, evidence-based practice, and genuine compassion. Whether you're a weekend warrior, a desk worker, a new mom, or a growing child, we provide the right care at every stage of life.

  • Doctor of Chiropractic (DC) — fully licensed and certified
  • Evidence-based, non-invasive treatment approach
  • Personalized care plans for every patient
  • Family-friendly clinic welcoming all ages
  • Direct insurance billing available for your convenience
