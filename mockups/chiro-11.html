About Us

A Higher Standard of Chiropractic Care in St. John's

At Higher State Health & Wellness Club, we believe that true health goes beyond simply masking symptoms. Our approach is rooted in understanding the root cause of your pain and developing a personalized chiropractic plan that restores proper spinal alignment, improves nervous system function, and empowers you with the tools to stay well long-term.

Located in the heart of St. John's, NL, our clinic combines the latest evidence-based chiropractic techniques with a genuinely caring team dedicated to helping every patient achieve their highest state of health. Whether you're dealing with chronic back pain, recovering from a sports injury, or seeking preventive wellness care, we're here to guide you every step of the way.

  • Evidence-based adjustments and corrective care protocols

  • Direct insurance billing for your convenience
  • Welcoming patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors
