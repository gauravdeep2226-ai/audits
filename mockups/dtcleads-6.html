About Us

Born from Athlete Frustration. Built for Real Recovery.

Mike Sullivan created CompResFit after years of dealing with inadequate recovery products that made big promises but delivered little. As a competitive athlete and fitness enthusiast, Mike knew there had to be a better way — products engineered with real compression science, not just marketed with it.

CompResFit was built to bridge the gap between clinical-grade compression therapy and everyday accessibility. Every product is rigorously tested for durability, comfort, and performance so athletes at every level can recover the way pros do — without the pro-team budget.

  • Science-backed graduated compression technology
  • Durable, sweat-wicking performance materials
  • Designed by athletes, for athletes
  • Trusted by runners, gym-goers, and weekend warriors
  • Satisfaction guarantee on every order
