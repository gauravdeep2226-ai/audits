About Us

Healthcare Rooted in Compassion & Clinical Excellence

Monastery Health is St. John's premier physiotherapy and healthcare clinic, located in the heart of the city at 63 Patrick Street. Our multidisciplinary team of registered physiotherapists, massage therapists, and healthcare professionals work collaboratively to deliver personalized care that goes beyond symptom relief — we treat the whole person.

We believe that every patient deserves individualized attention, honest communication, and a clear path to recovery. Whether you're dealing with a chronic condition, recovering from an injury, or simply wanting to move and feel better, our team is dedicated to helping you achieve lasting results through evidence-based practice and hands-on care.

  • Registered Physiotherapists & Massage Therapists on staff
  • Evidence-based, patient-centred treatment approach
  • Direct insurance billing for your convenience
  • Comprehensive assessments with personalized treatment plans
  • Welcoming, modern clinic in downtown St. John's
