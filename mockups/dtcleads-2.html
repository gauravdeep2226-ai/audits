About Us

Meet Shelly L. Gayton

Shelly L. Gayton founded Grounding Well with a singular mission: to help people stop surviving and start truly living. Drawing from years of study in energy healing, somatic practices, and holistic wellness, Shelly brings a deeply compassionate, whole-person approach to every session she leads.

At Grounding Well, we believe healing is not a luxury — it is a necessity. Whether you're navigating burnout, emotional heaviness, or a desire for deeper connection, Shelly meets you exactly where you are and walks alongside you toward clarity, balance, and lasting vitality.

  • Certified Holistic Wellness & Energy Healing Practitioner
  • Specialized training in grounding and somatic awareness techniques
  • Personalized, one-on-one sessions tailored to your unique journey
  • Safe, compassionate space for emotional and spiritual healing
  • Active community presence with a growing Facebook wellness group
