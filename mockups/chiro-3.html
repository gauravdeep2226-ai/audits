Your Journey

What to Expect

We believe in transparency and education. Here's exactly what happens from your first call to your ongoing wellness care.

1

Initial Consultation & Assessment

Your chiropractor will take a thorough health history, discuss your symptoms, and perform a comprehensive physical examination to understand the full picture of your spinal health.

2

Digital X-Ray & Diagnosis

Where clinically indicated, we use digital X-ray imaging to identify spinal misalignments, disc involvement, or structural irregularities — giving us a precise, evidence-based diagnosis.

3

Personalized Treatment Plan

We build a custom care plan with clear goals, frequency recommendations, and measurable outcomes. Your plan may include adjustments, soft tissue therapy, exercises, and lifestyle advice.

4

Ongoing Wellness Care

Once your acute pain is resolved, we help you maintain spinal health and prevent recurrence with periodic wellness visits, postural coaching, and movement strategies.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first chiropractic visit at St. John's Back Pain Clinic.

Your first appointment typically lasts 45–60 minutes. Your chiropractor will review your health history, conduct a thorough physical and spinal examination, and may take digital X-rays if needed. You'll then receive an initial adjustment and a clear overview of your recommended treatment plan and expected outcomes.

The sound you hear during a spinal adjustment is called cavitation — it's simply the release of gas (nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide) from the fluid inside the joint as pressure changes. It's completely normal, painless, and a good indicator that the joint has been successfully mobilized. Not all adjustments produce this sound, and that's perfectly fine too.

Most patients notice a meaningful improvement in pain and mobility within 6–10 visits, though many feel better after just 2–3. The number of visits depends on the severity and chronicity of your condition, your age, lifestyle factors, and how well you follow your home exercise recommendations. We'll give you a realistic timeline at your first appointment.

Yes, most major Canadian extended health benefit plans cover chiropractic care. This includes Blue Cross, Manulife, Sun Life, Canada Life, GreenShield, and others. We also accept MVA (motor vehicle accident) and Workers' Compensation Board claims. We recommend confirming your specific coverage limits with your provider before your first visit.

Chiropractic care is widely recognized as one of the safest non-invasive, drug-free healthcare options for musculoskeletal conditions. It is safe for patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors. Our chiropractors perform a thorough health screening before any treatment to identify any contraindications and choose the most appropriate, gentlest techniques for your specific situation.
