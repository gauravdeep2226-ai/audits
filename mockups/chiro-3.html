About Us

Newfoundland's Most Trusted Chiropractic Clinic

St. John's Back Pain Clinic has been a cornerstone of spinal health in Newfoundland and Labrador for years. Our licensed Doctors of Chiropractic combine thorough clinical assessment with hands-on, evidence-based treatment to address the root cause of your pain — not just the symptoms.

We believe every patient deserves a personalized care plan. Whether you're dealing with a long-standing spinal condition, recovering from an injury, or simply want to move better and feel stronger, our team is dedicated to guiding you toward lasting relief and improved quality of life.
