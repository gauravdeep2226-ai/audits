About Us

Dr. Ken Budgell & the Back Pain Clinic

At the Back Pain Clinic in St. John's, NL, Dr. Ken Budgell combines years of clinical expertise with a genuine passion for helping patients live pain-free. His philosophy is simple: address the root cause, not just the symptoms. Through thorough assessment and evidence-informed chiropractic techniques, Dr. Budgell develops individualized care plans that deliver lasting results — not just temporary relief.

Whether you're a desk worker battling chronic lower back pain, a weekend athlete recovering from injury, or a parent seeking gentle care for your child, the Back Pain Clinic offers a welcoming, professional environment where every patient receives the time and attention they deserve. Insurance billing is available, making quality chiropractic care accessible to everyone in the community.

  • Licensed Doctor of Chiropractic (D.C.), Memorial University-affiliated training
  • 20+ years of clinical experience treating all ages in St. John's
  • Evidence-based, patient-centred approach to spinal health
  • Direct insurance billing for your convenience
  • Comprehensive initial assessments for accurate diagnosis and treatment
