About Us

Your St. John's Chiropractor — Dr. Ken Budgell

At the Back Pain Clinic, Dr. Ken Budgell is dedicated to one purpose: helping the people of St. John's live pain-free, active lives. With years of clinical experience and a passion for evidence-informed chiropractic care, Dr. Budgell takes the time to understand the root cause of your pain — not just the symptoms — and builds a treatment plan that delivers lasting results.

Located conveniently at 1 Anderson Ave in St. John's, NL, the clinic serves patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors, athletes to office workers. We believe that your spine is the foundation of your health, and our goal is to keep that foundation strong so you can live your best life.
