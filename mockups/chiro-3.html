About Us

St. John's Most Trusted Chiropractic Clinic

Dr. Ken Budgell is a registered Doctor of Chiropractic serving the St. John's community from his clinic at 1 Anderson Avenue. With a deep commitment to evidence-based care, Dr. Budgell combines precise spinal adjustments with rehabilitative exercises and patient education to deliver lasting, measurable results — not just short-term pain relief.

At Back Pain Clinic, the philosophy is simple: every patient deserves a thorough assessment, an honest diagnosis and a personalized treatment plan. Whether you're dealing with a recent injury, chronic spinal pain or a performance-limiting condition, Dr. Budgell works collaboratively with you to restore function, eliminate pain and prevent recurrence.
