About Us

Chiropractic Care Built on Results, Not Just Relief

At Echelon Wellness St. John's, we believe that true health starts with a properly functioning spine and nervous system. Our licensed Doctors of Chiropractic take a thorough, evidence-informed approach — combining precise spinal analysis with hands-on treatment to address the root cause of your pain, not just the symptoms.

Serving the Mount Pearl and greater St. John's community, our clinic is committed to delivering exceptional patient outcomes in a warm, professional environment. Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or simply looking to move and feel better, our team is here for every step of your wellness journey.

  • Licensed Doctors of Chiropractic (DC) registered in Newfoundland
  • Evidence-based treatment protocols for lasting results
  • Direct insurance billing for your convenience
  • Personalized treatment plans tailored to your goals
  • Welcoming clinic with flexible appointment times
