About Us

St. John's Most Trusted Chiropractic Clinic

At St. John's Back Pain Clinic, we believe that a healthy spine is the foundation of a healthy life. For years, our dedicated team of licensed chiropractors has served the Newfoundland community with compassionate, evidence-based care — helping patients of all ages move better, feel stronger, and live pain-free.

We take a thorough, whole-person approach to every patient. That means detailed initial assessments, customized treatment plans, and ongoing education so you understand your condition and play an active role in your own recovery. We don't just treat symptoms — we get to the root cause.

  • Licensed Doctors of Chiropractic (DC) registered with the Newfoundland & Labrador Chiropractic Board
  • Direct billing to most major insurance providers — no paperwork hassle
  • Comprehensive initial assessments with digital postural analysis
  • Evidence-informed treatment protocols for lasting, measurable results
  • Welcoming clinic environment for patients of all ages, from infants to seniors
