About Us

Healing Hands, Personalized Care

Samantha Lyver-Foley is a Registered Massage Therapist serving the Mount Pearl and greater St. John's community in Newfoundland and Labrador. With a deep commitment to evidence-informed practice, Samantha tailors every treatment to the individual — whether you're recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or simply seeking relief from daily stress.

Samantha believes that therapeutic massage is not a luxury — it's an essential part of your overall health and wellness. From athletes pushing their limits to new mothers navigating the changes of pregnancy, every patient receives compassionate, results-driven care in a welcoming, professional environment.

  • Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) — fully licensed and insured
  • Direct billing available with most major insurance providers
  • Individualized treatment plans for every patient
  • Experienced with prenatal, sports, and chronic pain populations
  • Conveniently located in Mount Pearl, NL — easy parking available
