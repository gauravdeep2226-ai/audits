About Us

St. John's Most Trusted Health & Wellness Clinic

Ascend Health Physiotherapy was founded on a simple belief: every person deserves to live a life free from pain. Located in the heart of St. John's, NL, our clinic brings together a passionate team of registered physiotherapists, chiropractors, and wellness professionals committed to delivering exceptional, evidence-based care.

We take a whole-body approach to your health — digging deep to identify the root cause of your pain, not just masking the symptoms. Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing a chronic condition, or looking to optimize your performance, our team builds a personalized treatment plan designed around your unique goals and lifestyle.
