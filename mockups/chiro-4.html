About Us

Newfoundland's Professional Chiropractic Community

The NL Chiropractic Association is the professional organization representing licensed Doctors of Chiropractic across Newfoundland and Labrador. Our mission is to advance the chiropractic profession, ensure the highest standards of patient care, and connect residents with qualified practitioners throughout the province.

We believe that spinal health is foundational to overall wellness. Our member chiropractors use evidence-based techniques — from traditional spinal manipulation to modern rehabilitative protocols — to help patients recover from injury, manage chronic conditions, and achieve lasting pain relief without relying on medication or surgery.

  • All members are licensed Doctors of Chiropractic (DC)
  • Regulated by the Newfoundland & Labrador Chiropractic Board
  • Evidence-based, patient-centered approach to spinal care
  • Direct insurance billing with major providers
  • Serving Newfoundland & Labrador communities province-wide
