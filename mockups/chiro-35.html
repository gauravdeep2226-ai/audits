About Us

St. John's Most Trusted Chiropractic Team

At InMotion Health Centre Inc, we believe that optimal health is built on a foundation of proper spinal function and an active lifestyle. Our licensed Doctors of Chiropractic take the time to understand your unique history, goals, and challenges — delivering personalized care plans that go beyond temporary relief to address the root cause of your pain.
