About Us

Care Rooted in Insight, Driven by Results

At Core Insight Chiropractic, we believe that true healing begins with understanding the root cause of your pain — not just masking symptoms. Located in the heart of St. John's, NL, our practice combines cutting-edge chiropractic techniques with a deeply patient-centered approach that prioritizes your long-term wellbeing.

From your very first visit, we conduct a thorough assessment to understand your health history, lifestyle, and goals. Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or seeking to optimize your performance and posture, we build a personalized care plan designed to deliver lasting relief and empower you to thrive.

  • Licensed Doctor of Chiropractic (DC) — fully regulated in Newfoundland & Labrador
  • Evidence-based techniques including Diversified, Activator, and Thompson methods
  • Direct insurance billing for most major health benefit plans
  • Comprehensive initial assessments with orthopedic and neurological testing
  • Patient education and home exercise programs included with every care plan
