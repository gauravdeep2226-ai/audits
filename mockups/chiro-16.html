About Us

Chiropractic Care With a Coastal Heart

At Coastal Chiropractic, we believe that true wellness starts with a healthy, well-functioning spine. Located in the heart of St. John's, NL, our clinic combines evidence-based chiropractic techniques with a warm, patient-first approach to help individuals and families of all ages live pain-free, active lives on the beautiful Avalon Peninsula.

Our Doctor of Chiropractic brings years of hands-on clinical experience and ongoing professional development to every appointment. Whether you're dealing with a chronic condition, a recent injury, or simply want to optimize your spinal health, we create a personalized care plan that fits your life — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

  • Licensed Doctor of Chiropractic (DC), NBCE Board Certified
  • 5-star rated care trusted by St. John's families
  • Evidence-informed techniques including Diversified, Activator & Webster
  • Direct insurance billing for your convenience
  • Treating patients from infants to seniors with tailored protocols
