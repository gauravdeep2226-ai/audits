About Us

Dr. John Paul Gordon & Body Quest Health & Wellness

At Body Quest Health & Wellness, Dr. John Paul Gordon brings a patient-first philosophy to every adjustment. He believes the body has an extraordinary ability to heal itself when the spine and nervous system are functioning at their best. His approach combines evidence-based chiropractic techniques with a genuine commitment to understanding each patient's unique health goals.

Serving the St. John's community from the clinic on O'Leary Ave, Dr. Gordon and his team take the time to listen, educate, and empower patients to take control of their health — not just treat symptoms, but address the root causes of pain and dysfunction for lasting results.

  • Doctor of Chiropractic (DC) — fully licensed in Newfoundland & Labrador
  • Evidence-based spinal manipulation and soft-tissue techniques
  • 5-star rated by patients across the St. John's community
  • Insurance billing for most major benefit providers
  • Personalized treatment plans for every age and condition
