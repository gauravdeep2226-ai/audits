About Us

St. John's Premier Wellness Destination

Winterholme Wellness Center & Spa has been a trusted name in therapeutic care and luxury relaxation in St. John's, Newfoundland. Housed in the beautifully restored Winterholme Heritage Inn, our spa combines historic elegance with modern wellness practices to create an experience that is truly one of a kind.

Our team of Registered Massage Therapists brings compassion, expertise, and a genuine commitment to your health and recovery. Whether you're seeking relief from chronic pain, recovering from an injury, managing stress, or simply treating yourself to a well-deserved escape, we are here to support every step of your wellness journey.

  • Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) on staff
  • Extended health insurance direct billing available
  • Serene, beautifully appointed treatment rooms
  • Customized sessions tailored to your health goals
  • Conveniently located in historic downtown St. John's
