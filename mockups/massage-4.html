About Us

St. John's Premier Wellness Retreat

Nestled in the historic heart of St. John's, Newfoundland, Winterholme Wellness Center & Spa has been a sanctuary of healing and relaxation for locals and visitors alike. Our beautiful Victorian-era setting provides the perfect backdrop for a truly transformative wellness experience.

Our team of Registered Massage Therapists brings deep expertise, compassion, and a commitment to your health. Whether you're recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or simply in need of restorative rest, we customize every session to meet your unique goals — mind, body, and soul.

  • Registered Massage Therapists (RMTs) on staff
  • Direct billing and insurance accepted
  • Historic, beautifully appointed spa setting
  • Comprehensive range of therapeutic treatments
  • Personalized care plans for every client
