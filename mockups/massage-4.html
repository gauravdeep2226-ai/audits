About Us

St. John's Destination for Genuine Healing

Winterholme Wellness Center & Spa is a cherished retreat nestled in the character-rich setting of 79 Rennies Mill Rd in downtown St. John's. Our historic surroundings provide the perfect backdrop for a wellness experience that honors both tradition and modern therapeutic science. For years, we have been the trusted choice for Newfoundlanders seeking authentic, results-driven care.

Our team of Registered Massage Therapists brings deep clinical expertise and a genuine passion for healing. Every session begins with a thorough consultation to understand your goals — whether that's pain relief, stress reduction, athletic recovery, or simply a restorative escape. We take pride in personalizing every treatment so you leave feeling your very best.

  • Registered Massage Therapists (RMT) on staff
  • Direct insurance billing available
  • Tranquil, historic Victorian setting
  • Personalized treatment plans for every client
  • Serving St. John's and surrounding communities
