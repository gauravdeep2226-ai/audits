Issues Found

What's costing you appointment bookings

Three measured problems pulling customers away before they can schedule.

⏳
Largest Contentful Paint takes 10.6 seconds
High Impact

Pet owners searching "emergency pet dentist near me" see a blank screen for over 10 seconds before your main content loads. Most will click the next clinic's result. On mobile, this delay means lost appointment requests.

📦
272 KB of unused JavaScript slowing down mobile
High Impact

Your site loads 92 KB of JavaScript code that never runs, plus 44 KB of unused CSS. On a pet owner's phone over cellular, that's extra seconds burned before the appointment form appears. Every second of delay costs bookings.

🔍
Missing meta description in Google results
Medium Impact

Google has no description to show under your listing in local search results. Without a compelling 160-character description, fewer pet owners click through from "pet dentist [city]" searches. This directly reduces traffic to your booking page.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted fixes, ranked by appointment booking impact.

⚡
Eliminate unused code and defer non-critical JavaScript

Remove the 92 KB of unused JavaScript and 44 KB of unused CSS. Defer Shopify secondary scripts and analytics—they don't need to load before the booking form. Cache static assets server-side (savings: 126 KB).

Expected impact
↑ 55% faster mobile load
🎯
Optimize Largest Contentful Paint to under 3 seconds

Lazy-load below-fold images. Preload the hero image or critical appointment-booking section. Compress images server-side. Reduce the total page payload (currently 2.5 MB) by splitting code per page type.

Expected impact
↑ More completed bookings
📝
Add meta descriptions to all pages

Write 160-character meta descriptions for your home page and service pages—highlight emergency care, hours, and "book online" CTA. This increases click-through rate from local search by 20–30% and signals to Google you're an active practice.

Expected impact
↑ More search traffic
