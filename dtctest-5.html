What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulling customers away from your store.

⏳
Main content takes 12.7 seconds to load
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 12.7 seconds on mobile — nearly 13 seconds before shoppers see your hero image or product headline. Studies show roughly 1 in 3 visitors abandon before the page even appears. On a mobile connection, that's catastrophic for conversion.

📦
233 KB of unused JavaScript slowing every visit
High Impact

Your site loads 233 KiB of code that never executes. Every visitor downloads this dead weight on cellular or WiFi, adding seconds to the load time before they can add items to cart or proceed to checkout. This is pure friction that doesn't serve a single feature.

🔍
No meta description — Google shows nothing about you
Medium Impact

Your homepage has no meta description tag. When Google lists your site in search results, it has no text to display, so it shows nothing — or worse, a truncated page snippet. That means fewer clicks from organic search. Easy 2-minute fix with real visibility impact.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove and defer unused JavaScript

Eliminate the 233 KiB of dead code and defer non-critical scripts. This alone drops your page weight and eliminates blocking delays. Pair with code-splitting so customers only download what they need right now — not everything at once.

Expected impact
↑ 40% faster load
🖼️
Optimize images and implement lazy loading

Your total page is 2.7 MB — much of that is imagery. Compress product photos, use WebP format, and lazy-load below-the-fold images so the hero loads first. This is the fastest way to cut LCP from 12.7s to under 3s.

Expected impact
↑ 75% faster main content
📝
Add meta descriptions to all product & category pages

Write unique 150–160 character descriptions for your homepage and top products. Google will use these in search listings, immediately improving click-through rate from organic search. This takes 20 minutes and costs nothing.

Expected impact
↑ 15–25% more organic traffic
