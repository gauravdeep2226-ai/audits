What's costing you patient appointments

Three measured problems pulling qualified leads away from your practice.

Main content takes 13.9 seconds to load
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 13.9 seconds on mobile — patients searching "oncologist near me" are gone long before your practice information or appointment button appears. This directly costs you qualified leads before they can call or book.

🚀
Unused JavaScript inflates page size by 278 KB
High Impact

Your site loads 278 KB of JavaScript that never runs. On mobile cellular networks, this wasted payload adds 3–5 seconds to your page load time. Patients on 4G see a blank screen while your site processes code for features you don't use.

🔍
No meta description for Google
Medium Impact

Google has no description to show under your listing in search results — potential patients see only your title. A clear, enticing meta description increases click-through rate by 5–8% without any traffic cost, directly putting more calls in your calendar.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact on bookings and visibility.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer non-critical scripts

Strip the 278 KB of unused code and split remaining scripts into critical (above-fold) and deferred (after page load). This eliminates render-blocking and drops your LCP from 13.9s to under 4s, ensuring patients see your contact form before they bounce.

Expected impact
↑ 70% faster on mobile
📦
Cache static assets and remove unused CSS (44 KB)

Enable browser caching for images, fonts, and stylesheets, and eliminate 44 KB of unused CSS rules. Combined with lazy-loading images below the fold, you'll drop page weight from 3,076 KB to under 2,400 KB. Repeat visitors see instant loads.

Expected impact
↑ 65% faster return visits
🎯
Write a clinical meta description

Add a 155-character meta description highlighting your specialty, board certification, and ease of booking. Google will display this under your title in search results, increasing the click-through rate from patients actively seeking an oncologist in your area.

Expected impact
↑ 5–8% more patient clicks
