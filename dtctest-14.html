Issues Found

What's stopping conversions

Three measured problems pulling revenue from your DTC store.

⏱️
Massive images block checkout for 8.4 seconds
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 8.4 seconds on mobile. One in five shoppers abandon before your product images and Add to Cart button even appear. On a typical mobile connection, customers give up before they see what they're buying.

📦
648 KB of unused JavaScript slowing every page load
High Impact

Your site downloads 648 KB of JavaScript that never runs on the homepage. This dead weight delays the initial render by seconds—pushing your First Contentful Paint to 3.7 seconds. Every byte deleted means faster checkout and higher conversion.

🖼️
Product images missing alt text, hurting SEO visibility
Medium Impact

Your image alt text score is 0—Google can't read what's in your product photos. This kills image search traffic and makes your listings invisible in visual search. Competitors with proper alt text are stealing organic traffic you should own.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🎯
Compress and lazy-load product images

Switch to modern image formats (WebP), compress aggressively, and defer off-screen images. This alone cuts your LCP from 8.4s to under 3s. Customers see products faster and reach checkout before decision fatigue sets in.

Expected impact
↑ 60% faster checkout load
🧹
Remove or code-split the 648 KB of unused JS

Audit your script dependencies and remove dead code. If features are conditional, load them on-demand. This drops your FCP from 3.7s to under 2s and ensures the Add to Cart button is interactive immediately.

Expected impact
↑ 40% faster first paint
🔍
Add descriptive alt text to all product images

Write unique, keyword-rich alt text for every product photo (e.g. "ViraRoot natural immunity supplement—bottle and capsules"). This unlocks Google image search and makes your products discoverable to customers searching visually or via voice.

Expected impact
↑ Image search visibility
