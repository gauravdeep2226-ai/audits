What's costing you consultations

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

Main content takes 10.4 seconds to appear on mobile
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint (the moment your hero image or main message loads) is 10.4 seconds on mobile. A potential client searching for dog nutrition advice will abandon and call a competitor before your value proposition appears on screen.

📦
2.1 MB of unused JavaScript slows every page load
High Impact

Your site loads 2,100 KiB of JavaScript that never runs. On cellular networks—where most consultations are booked—this is wasted seconds of download and parsing time before your contact form or booking CTA becomes interactive.

🔴
JavaScript blocks the main thread for 3.4 seconds
High Impact

Your Total Blocking Time is 3,390 ms. This means visitors cannot tap your phone number, click a booking button, or scroll through your packages for over 3 seconds after the page appears to load. Many will give up and leave.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

✂️
Remove or defer unused 2.1 MB of JavaScript

Audit your vendor scripts (Clarity, TriplePixel, etc.) and lazy-load or remove non-critical bundles. Ship only the JavaScript required for contact forms, click-to-call, and initial page render. This alone can cut Time to Interactive by 40–50%.

Expected impact
↑ 45% faster interactions
🖼️
Optimize and lazy-load hero images

Your LCP is 10.4s—likely due to a large unoptimized hero image. Convert to WebP, use responsive sizes, and lazy-load below-the-fold images. Preload your primary hero image to bring LCP under 2.5s.

Expected impact
↓ LCP to ~2.5s
💾
Enable browser caching and compress assets

Implement long-lived cache headers for CSS and fonts. Enable gzip compression (you can save ~1.1 MB). Minify unused CSS (80 KiB savings available). These reduce total page weight from 8.2 MB to ~6 MB, cutting repeat visits by 30%.

Expected impact
↑ 26% faster repeats
