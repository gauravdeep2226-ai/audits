Issues Found

Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Your mobile site is loading critical resources too slowly
High Impact

Your site has render-blocking JavaScript and stylesheets that delay when visitors see your menu, hours, and phone number. On a phone over cellular data, this delay adds up — 53% of mobile visitors abandon after 3 seconds. That's a hungry customer tapping the restaurant below you in Google instead of waiting for your site to load.

🔍
Google can't describe what you offer in search results
High Impact

Your meta description is missing or incomplete. When someone searches "restaurants near me" or "bars near me" on their phone, Google has no summary to show under your name — so your competitor's listing (which shows "Reserve now — live sports, daily specials") gets the tap instead. You're losing visibility before visitors even click.

⚙️
Your site loads JavaScript code that never runs
Medium Impact

Wix bundles polyfills, legacy compatibility code, and analytics that your modern phone browsers don't need. On cellular data, these unused scripts delay your menu from appearing. Every extra second lost is a customer who walks to the bar next door instead of checking your menu and reservation system first.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Defer render-blocking resources and use async loading

Move JavaScript to load after your menu, hours, and contact info appear. Inline critical CSS so visitors see your hero image and key information instantly — even on slow 3G. This is the single fastest way to keep hungry customers on your page long enough to check your specials and make a reservation.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
📝
Add a compelling meta description and improve SEO tags

Write a 155-character description that appears under your listing: "Gaggling Goose — Live sports, craft cocktails, reservation system. Book a table." Add structured data (schema.org markup) so Google knows you're a restaurant with hours, location, and phone. This tells Google exactly what you offer and gets you in front of "restaurants near me" searches when FIFA fans are looking right now.

Expected impact
↑ Top result for "bars near me" and "restaurants near me"
🗑️
Remove unused JavaScript bundles and polyfills

Wix includes legacy polyfills for old browsers that don't matter on mobile. Work with your hosting provider to strip code that never runs — this saves 100+ KB of downloads on cellular. Faster load = more customers get past your site's front door instead of bouncing to a faster competitor.

Expected impact
↑ More walk-ins from "restaurants near me" searches
