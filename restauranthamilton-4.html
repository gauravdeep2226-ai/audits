Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

Mobile experience is dangerously slow
High Impact

Your mobile score of 55 means half your customers are hitting a broken experience. When someone on the sidewalk or searching on their phone lands on your site, they're waiting too long to see your menu, hours, and phone number. 53% of mobile visitors abandon after 3 seconds — that's a hungry person tapping your competitor instead.

Google can't fully describe your restaurant in search results
High Impact

Your SEO score is 0.92, but key signals are missing or weak. When someone searches "Vietnamese restaurants near me" or "pho Hamilton," Google has limited information to show under your listing — while competitors display reserve buttons, hours, and menus right in the results. They get the click; you get passed over.

⚡
Render-blocking resources delay your menu from loading
High Impact

Your site makes visitors wait while invisible files load before they can even see your menu. On cellular data — which 80%+ of restaurant searches happen on — this delay is the difference between a customer seeing your specials and deciding to walk to the restaurant next door that loads faster.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚙️
Eliminate render-blocking JavaScript and optimize critical assets

Defer non-critical scripts, inline above-the-fold CSS, and use modern image formats with lazy loading. This cuts the time between landing and seeing your menu from seconds to milliseconds — the difference between a customer deciding to dine in or scrolling to your competitor.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads instantly on mobile
🎯
Strengthen SEO signals with complete meta tags and structured data

Ensure all pages have unique, keyword-rich meta descriptions under 160 characters, optimize document titles with location + cuisine, and add schema markup for restaurant hours, ratings, and menu. When someone searches for Vietnamese food in Hamilton, Google will show your reserve button and hours — not a blank listing.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me"
📲
Redesign for mobile-first with faster, leaner codebase

Move from a score of 55 to 90+ on mobile by reducing total page weight, compressing images, and using code splitting. With FIFA 2026 bringing thousands of tourists searching "restaurants near Hamilton" on their phones right now, every second counts. A fast mobile site converts walk-ins and reservations; a slow one sends them to your competitors.

Expected impact
↑ More walk-ins from Google and mobile searches
