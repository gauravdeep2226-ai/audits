What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

Massive content delay — visitors abandon before checkout loads
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 9.5 seconds on mobile. Shoppers see a blank screen for nearly 10 seconds — long enough to jump to a competitor. Every second of delay costs conversions.

📦
457 KB of unused JavaScript blocking page load
High Impact

Your site loads code that never runs, adding 457 KiB of wasted bytes. On 4G mobile, that's precious seconds wasted before the Add to Cart button even appears. Visitors with slow connections give up.

🔍
No meta description — Google has no reason to show you in search
Medium Impact

Your homepage has no meta description (scoring 0 on this element). When someone searches for your products, Google shows no preview text under your link — fewer clicks and lost traffic you should own.
