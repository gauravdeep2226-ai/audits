Issues Found

What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱
Largest Contentful Paint takes 9.7 seconds
High Impact

On mobile over 4G, customers see a blank screen for nearly 10 seconds before your main product image or headline loads. By then, 1 in 3 shoppers have already left for a faster competitor. This delay directly kills your conversion rate.

📦
210 KB of unused JavaScript is dragging your site down
High Impact

Your site loads scripts that never run. On cellular, this wasted code delays your Add to Cart button by seconds. Removing it would free up enough bandwidth to speed up your checkout flow and reduce abandonment.

🔍
Google has no meta description for your homepage
High Impact

When people search for your products, Google shows no preview under your listing — just a URL. No meta description means fewer clicks from search, even if you rank. This is costing you qualified traffic.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and defer non-critical scripts

Strip out the 210 KB of unused JavaScript and defer scripts that don't block initial render. Cache static assets more aggressively (158 KB of savings available). This alone shaves 3–4 seconds off your Largest Contentful Paint on mobile.

Expected impact
↑ 40% faster load
🖼
Lazy-load product images and optimize their format

Your total page weight is 4,172 KB — most of it images. Implement lazy-loading so only above-the-fold images load first, and convert large PNGs to WebP. Your Speed Index (currently 6.7s) will drop sharply, and checkout flows will feel instant.

Expected impact
↑ More checkouts
📝
Add a compelling meta description to your homepage

Write a 155-character meta description that includes your brand name and top value prop (e.g., "PEAK NXT — Premium supplements for fitness. Fast shipping, lab-tested quality."). Google will display this under your listing, increasing click-through rate from search.

Expected impact
↑ 12–18% more clicks
