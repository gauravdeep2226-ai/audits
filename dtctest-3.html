Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulling revenue from your store.

⏱️
Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) takes 19.1 seconds on mobile
High Impact

Your main product images and content don't load until nearly 20 seconds have passed on a typical phone. In ecommerce, every second of delay costs conversions—roughly 1 in 5 visitors abandon before your hero image appears. That's lost sales before customers see what you're selling.

⚙️
1.63 MB of unused JavaScript slowing every page load
High Impact

Your site is shipping 1,630 KB of code that never runs on the homepage, yet that bloat forces every visitor—especially mobile shoppers on slow connections—to download and parse it. This unused code alone accounts for significant delay before the Add to Cart button becomes interactive.

🔍
Meta descriptions missing—Google has no sales copy for your listings
High Impact

Meta descriptions show as 0 in your SEO audit, meaning Google displays a generic snippet or nothing under your search results. Without compelling preview text, fewer searchers click through. For a store, that's lost organic traffic and lost revenue at the most critical moment—when someone is actively shopping.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact to revenue.

🖼️
Lazy-load product images and defer non-critical JavaScript

Implement aggressive image lazy-loading so only above-the-fold visuals load immediately, and split JavaScript into critical (cart, checkout) and deferred bundles. Use modern image formats (WebP) and responsive sizing to shrink transfers. Combined with server caching, this can cut LCP from 19s to under 3s.

Expected impact
↑ 85% faster pages
🧹
Audit and remove 1.63 MB of unused JavaScript bundles

Analyze which npm packages and bundled features are actually used on each page template. Remove duplicate polyfills, unused analytics, and third-party scripts that don't serve the core shopping flow. A code audit typically uncovers 30–50% waste in Shopify stores.

Expected impact
↑ 20% faster downloads
📝
Write unique meta descriptions for all key product categories

Add 120–160 character descriptions to your homepage and top category pages, highlighting your strongest value (e.g., "Shop premium men's and women's shoes. Free shipping on orders over $75. Fast delivery."). Google will display these snippets, driving higher click-through rates and stronger organic traffic to high-margin product lines.

Expected impact
↑ 15–25% organic clicks
