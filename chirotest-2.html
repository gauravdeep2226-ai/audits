Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📱
Mobile experience scores 61 — below the threshold where customers stay
High Impact

A score of 61 on mobile means your site is in the bottom 30% of restaurants. When someone on their phone searches "restaurants near me" right now during FIFA 2026, Google prioritizes faster sites. You're being buried in local results while competitors with scores above 75 get the tap. Those hungry customers never even see your listing.

🔍
SEO score of 0.92 — missing critical visibility elements
High Impact

Your SEO score sits at 0.92 (where 1.0 is perfect), which means Google is not showing your restaurant with full information when people search locally. Without a complete meta description, hours, and structured data, potential customers see a competitor's listing instead of yours — and that lost search impression is a lost reservation. Every day this week, FIFA tourists are searching your area on mobile and you're invisible.

⚙️
Wix platform loads heavy invisible code that slows your menu
Medium Impact

Your site is built on Wix, which loads dozens of render-blocking scripts before your menu and reservation button appear. A hungry person standing outside on their phone can't see your hours or book a table because the page is still loading invisible framework code. On slower cellular networks, this adds 2–3 seconds of blank screen time. By then, they've already scrolled to the next restaurant.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🚀
Migrate to a performance-first restaurant platform or custom build

Wix's architecture adds unnecessary weight. Moving to a dedicated restaurant platform (Toast, Square Online, or a custom Next.js build) cuts mobile load time in half and gets your Google ranking to above 80+. Your menu, hours, and "Reserve Now" button load in under 2 seconds instead of 5–6. That's the difference between a customer booking a table or walking next door.

Expected impact
↑ Mobile score jumps to 80+, menu loads in under 2 seconds
📍
Add complete local SEO schema and meta descriptions

Right now, when someone searches "best bars near me" or "restaurants in your city," Google has incomplete data about your business. Adding Schema.org LocalBusiness markup, full meta descriptions, and your hours/phone number to the page head brings your SEO score to 1.0 and gets you featured in Google's local pack with a description. That's the prime real estate — first 3 results — where FIFA fans and walk-ins look first.

Expected impact
↑ First result for "restaurants near me" — Google shows your hours and menu link
📸
Optimize images and lazy-load off-screen content

Your food photos and menu images are likely unoptimized and loading all at once, adding 100+ KB to page weight. Serving WebP/AVIF formats, compressing images, and lazy-loading anything below the fold cuts visual load time by 40%. Your menu thumbnail and hero image appear instantly, giving customers confidence to stay and browse instead of bouncing back to Google.

Expected impact
↑ More walk-ins from Google — customers see your food photos first
