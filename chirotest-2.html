What's costing you patient bookings

Three measured problems pulling patients away before they can schedule an appointment.

⏱️
Main content takes 10.6 seconds to appear on mobile
High Impact

Your First Contentful Paint is 10.6 seconds — patients searching "chiropractor Thunder Bay" on mobile stare at a blank screen for over 10 seconds. Most will call a competitor before your page even loads. This delays the moment your booking button becomes visible.

🖼️
Unused CSS (678 KB) and JavaScript (282 KB) bloat your page
High Impact

Your site ships 960 KB of code and styling that never runs. On a typical cellular connection, that dead weight adds 3–5 seconds to load time alone. Patients on 4G are waiting for assets that serve no purpose — every second lost is a booking lost to a faster clinic site.

📦
Total page weight is 3.4 MB — far too heavy for mobile
High Impact

Your homepage is 3,394 KB total. Combined with the unused CSS/JS, you're forcing patients to download assets equivalent to a small app just to see your booking form. On slower networks, this balloons load time and increases bounce rate before they reach your contact button.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact on patient bookings.

✂️
Remove 678 KB of unused CSS and 282 KB of unused JavaScript

Audit your stylesheet and script bundles to identify dead code. Most of your CSS is likely from old page templates or unused components. Removing these unused assets shrinks your total page weight by 28% and delivers content to patients 3–4 seconds faster on mobile.

Expected impact
↑ 35% faster page load
🎯
Optimize and lazy-load images; leverage browser caching for 660 KB savings

Your images and unoptimized assets account for ~660 KB in potential cache savings. Serve images in modern formats (WebP), resize to viewport, and defer off-screen images. This alone will cut your page weight by 19% and allow your booking button to load within 6–7 seconds on 4G.

Expected impact
↑ More patients see booking button
⚡
Defer non-critical JavaScript and inline critical rendering path

Move analytics, tracking, and interactive scripts to load after the main content appears. Inline your critical CSS so the booking button area renders instantly. This reduces your Largest Contentful Paint from 15.3s to under 5s on mobile, matching patient expectations and reducing abandonment.

Expected impact
↑ 66% faster core content
