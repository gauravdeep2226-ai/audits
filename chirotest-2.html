What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas costing you patient bookings

⏱️
Critical Load Time — Patients Bounce Before Booking
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) is 15.3 seconds on mobile. When a patient searches "chiropractor near me" on their phone and lands on your site, they stare at a blank screen for over 15 seconds before your main content appears. By that point, they've already clicked away to the next clinic. On a slow 4G connection, a prospective patient will abandon your site before they ever see your booking button or call-to-action.

📦
Bloated Code Slowing Cellular Networks
High Impact

Your site is loading 3,394 KB of total assets—3.3 MB. Of that, you're forcing browsers to load 282 KB of unused JavaScript and 678 KB of unused CSS. For a patient on cellular data in your Thunder Bay office or parking lot, that's wasted seconds and wasted data credits. These unnecessary resources are the primary culprit keeping your First Contentful Paint at 10.9 seconds.

🔗
Render-Blocking Resources Delaying Booking Visibility
High Impact

You have approximately 6,980 ms of render-blocking JavaScript and CSS that must finish processing before a patient can interact with your page. This means your booking button, your "Call Now" link, and your hours of operation are all invisible until the browser finishes parsing stylesheets and scripts. For a chiropractic practice, this delay translates directly into lost bookings and missed calls.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements to capture bookings before patients bounce

✂️
Remove Unused CSS and JavaScript Immediately

Eliminate the 282 KB of unused JavaScript and 678 KB of unused CSS that serves no purpose on your live pages. Audit your stylesheet imports and script dependencies—you likely have leftover code from old versions or plugins that aren't active. This single step reclaims roughly 960 KB of download bloat and removes thousands of milliseconds of parsing time that patients don't see.

Expected Impact
↑ 6.0 seconds faster load
⚡
Defer Non-Critical JavaScript and Inline Critical CSS

The 6,980 ms of render-blocking resources is the main bottleneck. Restructure your JavaScript to load asynchronously (defer attribute) so the page doesn't wait for analytics, chat widgets, or non-essential libraries. Inline the critical CSS needed to render the above-the-fold content (hero, hours, booking button) so patients see a functional page in under 2 seconds. This unblocks the browser and gets your CTA visible immediately.

Expected Impact
↑ 7.0 seconds faster interactivity
🖼️
Implement Aggressive Browser Caching (660 KB savings identified)

Google found 660 KB of assets that should be cached but aren't. Set proper cache headers on images, stylesheets, and fonts so repeat visitors (follow-up appointment checkers, returning patients) load the page instantly. Combined with the above fixes, your LCP on repeat visits will drop below 3 seconds, and your FCP will hit the 2.5s "good" threshold on mobile 4G networks. This removes friction from the booking path.

Expected Impact
↑ 2.0 seconds for return visits
