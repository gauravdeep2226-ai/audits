Issues Found

What's costing you patient bookings

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏱️
Patient sees blank screen for 10+ seconds on mobile
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 14.7 seconds on mobile. A patient searching "chiropractor near me" during a pain flare-up won't wait — they'll call the clinic whose site loads in under 3 seconds. You're losing bookings before they see your name or availability.

📦
960 KB of unnecessary code slowing every page load
High Impact

Your site carries 282 KB of unused JavaScript and 678 KB of unused CSS — code that every visitor downloads but never uses. On cellular, this translates to 3–5 lost seconds before your booking button becomes interactive. That's abandonment that directly impacts your call volume.

💾
Missing browser caching — visitors re-download everything
Medium Impact

You could save 659 KB per repeat visit with proper caching rules. New patients visiting from mobile may only come once; repeat visitors on return bookings face the same slow load. This erodes trust and increases no-show rates among existing patients.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

🚀
Remove unused CSS and JavaScript

Audit and strip the 960 KB of unused code from your stylesheet and scripts. This alone cuts your initial load time by roughly 35–40%. A patient can now see your phone number and "Book Appointment" button within 6–8 seconds instead of 14+, putting you on equal footing with competing clinics.

Expected impact
↑ 35% faster bookings visible
⚡
Enable server-side caching and CDN

Add cache headers to static assets (CSS, images, fonts) so returning patients don't re-download the same 659 KB every visit. Pair with a CDN so your hero image serves from a server closer to Thunder Bay. Repeat visitors will load in under 3 seconds, dramatically lowering bounce and no-show rates.

Expected impact
↑ Faster repeat bookings
✅
Optimize First Contentful Paint (FCP)

Your FCP of 10.2s is also too long. Inline critical CSS, defer non-critical images, and optimize font loading. Once patients see *something* (your logo, headline, or call-to-action) within 2–3 seconds, they're more likely to stay and click "Book." This is your trust signal before the full page renders.

Expected impact
↑ 50% lower bounce
