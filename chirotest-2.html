Issues Found

What's costing you sales

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⏳
Massive delay before products load
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 18.4 seconds on mobile — shoppers are staring at a blank screen for over 18 seconds before they see your first shoe. That's an eternity on a phone. Most abandon before the Add to Cart button even appears.

📦
1.7 MB of unused JavaScript slowing checkout
High Impact

Your site loads 1,733 KiB of JavaScript code that never runs on the homepage. On cellular, that's wasted seconds during the critical moment when a customer is deciding whether to click Add to Cart. Every second of delay costs you conversions.

🔍
Missing meta description — Google can't sell your site
High Impact

Your homepage has no meta description. Google is showing a generic placeholder in search results instead of a compelling summary of your shoe selection. Fewer clicks = fewer sales. Your SEO score is 0.92, but that 0 on meta description is a revenue leak.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

⚡
Code-split and defer non-critical JavaScript

Remove the 1.7 MB of unused JS from initial load. Only load scripts when customers interact with features. Pair this with image optimization and you cut LCP by 60–70%, getting products visible in under 6 seconds instead of 18.

Expected impact
↑ 11.8s faster load
💾
Enable browser caching and compress assets

You have 1,207 KiB of cacheable resources that aren't being cached. Set far-future cache headers and enable Gzip compression on CSS and JS. Returning visitors load 30–40% faster, and the site becomes snappier for first-time shoppers.

Expected impact
↑ 3.8s faster on repeat visits
🎯
Add meta description and optimize title tags

Write a compelling 155-character meta description highlighting your shoe selection, brands, and value prop. Your title is strong, but the missing description is leaving 15–20% of search clicks on the table. This costs almost nothing to fix and pays back immediately.

Expected impact
↑ 12–18% more clicks
