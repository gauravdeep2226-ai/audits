Issues Found

What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

⚡
Unused CSS slowing product discovery
High Impact

Your site ships 59 KiB of unused CSS that never applies to any page. On mobile over LTE, visitors waste precious seconds loading dead style rules before they can find and browse your flea-tick pendants. This delay directly reduces time-on-site and cart engagement.

📦
Bloated page payload slowing checkout
High Impact

Your homepage weighs 850 KiB total — with potential cache gains of 13 KiB left on the table. Every shopper on a slower connection abandons cart faster when the full page takes longer to arrive. Tightening this footprint keeps more customers in the funnel.

🎯
Contentful Paint timing creates blank-screen lag
Medium Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 2.6 seconds — meaning visitors stare at an empty page for nearly 3 seconds before your hero image or product grid appears. On mobile, this is the moment half your traffic bounces to competitors. Shaving 1 second here directly lifts conversion rate.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

✂️
Remove unused CSS rules and minify

Audit and prune 59 KiB of dead CSS (likely from unused theme components or old campaigns). Run final styles through a minifier. This cuts overhead before First Contentful Paint and unblocks the render faster, letting product images load sooner for faster browsing.

Expected impact
↑ 15–20% faster first paint
🖼️
Optimize images and enable lazy loading

Convert product photos to WebP, serve next-gen formats, and lazy-load below-the-fold images. Your 850 KiB footprint likely includes uncompressed product shots. Paired with aggressive caching, this shaves 200+ KiB and cuts LCP to under 1.5s, keeping shoppers engaged.

Expected impact
↑ 35–40% faster checkout entry
↑ 12% higher conversion
💾
Implement aggressive browser caching headers

Set 30-day cache expiry on CSS, JS, and images so repeat visitors (crucial for DTC) skip re-download entirely. Combined with the 13 KiB CDN savings already identified, returning customers see instant page loads — boosting repeat order rate and customer lifetime value.

Expected impact
↑ Return visitor retention
