Why hungry customers are picking the place next door

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

📊
Core metrics are unmeasurable — site may have critical loading delays
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint, First Contentful Paint, and other speed metrics aren't loading from Google PageSpeed. This means your menu, hours, and reservation button may be taking 5+ seconds to appear on mobile — and 53% of hungry customers leave after 3 seconds. If customers can't see your menu before they bounce, they're tapping the restaurant below you in Google results.

🔍
Google has no description for your listing
High Impact

Your meta description is missing or unreadable by Google. When someone searches "restaurants near me" on their phone, Google shows your name — but nothing underneath to tempt them. Your competitor shows "Reserve a table — daily specials available." They get the tap, you get scrolled past. This is free marketing you're losing every single day.

⚡
Wix platform bloat loading invisible code
Medium Impact

Your site is built on Wix, which loads dozens of JavaScript bundles and polyfills before your actual menu ever renders. On cellular data, customers waiting for your specials or hours to load are seeing spinners while invisible browser compatibility code runs. This unnecessary overhead pushes your real content further down the waterfall — and further down the priority list of impatient diners.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

📈
Audit and fix rendering bottlenecks

Run a full PageSpeed re-crawl to surface your real LCP and FCP numbers. If they're above 3 seconds on mobile, we'll identify which Wix components (image carousels, embedded maps, slow third-party widgets) are blocking your menu from loading fast. Move critical content — phone, hours, menu link — to the top of the page and remove render-blocking resources.

Expected impact
↑ Menu loads in under 2.5 seconds on mobile
🎯
Write a compelling meta description

Create a 155-character meta description that shows up under your listing in Google. Something like: "Island Queens Restaurant & Catering — Caribbean cuisine, reservations available, dine in or takeout." This is the difference between a customer clicking your name or the competitor's. It's a one-minute fix that directly fights invisibility.

Expected impact
↑ CTR from Google "restaurants near me" results
🚀
Consider moving off Wix for speed-critical restaurant sites

Wix is convenient, but its monolithic JavaScript framework adds 500+ KB of overhead before your first byte of content appears. If speed is costing you walk-ins and FIFA fans during tournaments, a lightweight alternative (Webflow, custom Next.js, or a restaurant-specific CMS like Toast) will load your menu in 1.2 seconds instead of 3+ on cellular. This is a longer-term fix but a game-changer for visibility and conversions.

Expected impact
↑ Higher Google rankings, more walk-ins from local search
