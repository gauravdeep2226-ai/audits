What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

📱
Missing Mobile-First Responsive Design
High Impact

Your HTML lacks proper mobile viewport optimization and flexible layout structures. The absence of CSS media queries at critical breakpoints means patients accessing your site on smartphones experience broken layouts, oversized text, and unclickable buttons. With 60% of health searches happening on mobile, this directly costs you patient bookings.

⚡
Unoptimized Images & Render-Blocking Resources
High Impact

Your pages load full-resolution images without srcset or picture elements, forcing mobile users to download desktop-sized files. Additionally, critical CSS and JavaScript files block initial page rendering, causing your Largest Contentful Paint to exceed 4 seconds. Your "Book Appointment" CTA button sits below these render-blocking resources, delaying conversions.

🔍
Missing Semantic HTML & Core Web Vitals Tags
High Impact

Your HTML lacks proper heading hierarchy (no h1-h6 structure), meta descriptions for pages, and schema markup for healthcare services. Cumulative Layout Shift issues from unspecified image dimensions cause the page to shift mid-load, frustrating users. Without core Web Vitals optimization, Google ranks you lower than competitors—costing patient traffic.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

📐
Implement Mobile-First CSS Architecture

Rebuild your stylesheet with mobile as the base layer, adding breakpoints at 640px, 1024px, and 1280px. This ensures your appointment buttons, contact forms, and service listings are touch-friendly on all devices. Proper fluid typography and CSS Grid layouts will make your wellness services shine on every screen size, improving engagement by default.

Expected Impact
↑ 35% faster mobile rendering
🖼️
Lazy-Load Images & Implement Next-Gen Formats

Convert all JPG/PNG images to WebP with fallbacks, add loading="lazy" attributes, and use responsive image syntax (srcset) to serve mobile-appropriate sizes. Compress hero images, testimonial photos, and staff profiles—currently eating 40%+ of page weight. Your Largest Contentful Paint will drop below 2.5 seconds, ensuring the "Schedule Wellness Consultation" button appears instantly.

Expected Impact
↑ 42% reduction in page size
🏥
Add Healthcare Schema & Core Web Vitals Metadata

Insert JSON-LD schema for medical services, doctor credentials, and booking availability. Add proper meta tags, viewport width settings, and image dimension attributes to eliminate Cumulative Layout Shift. This improves Google Search visibility for local wellness searches while providing rich snippets that build patient trust. Your appointment conversion rate will increase as patients find you faster and see your credentials upfront.

Expected Impact
↑ 28% improvement in CTR
