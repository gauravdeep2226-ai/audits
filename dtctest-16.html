Issues Found

What's keeping customers from discovering you

Three measured problems pulled from your live site.

🔍
Missing SEO Meta Descriptions
High Impact

Your homepage has zero meta descriptions set. Google has nothing to show under your listing in search results, so potential pet owners never see what your business offers. Without that description snippet, your click-through rate from organic search drops significantly.

⏱️
Slow Main Content Load (6.6 seconds)
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 6.6 seconds on mobile — customers stare at a mostly-blank screen for nearly 7 seconds before they see your main content. Mobile visitors abandon slow sites immediately. By the time your images and hero section render, they've already moved to a competitor's pet care site.

🖼️
Missing Image Alt Text (SEO & Accessibility)
High Impact

Your images lack descriptive alt text — Google cannot read what's in your product or service photos. This blinds both search engines (they can't rank you for "dog toys" or "pet supplies") and screen readers for accessibility. You're losing free organic visibility and excluding customers who rely on screen readers.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by impact.

📝
Write Meta Descriptions for Every Page

Add unique 155-character descriptions to your homepage and all product/service pages. Tell searchers exactly what you offer ("Premium pet supplies & grooming services for dogs"). Google will display these snippets in search results, directly boosting your click-through rate from organic traffic.

Expected impact
↑ 20–30% more clicks from search
🚀
Optimize Image Delivery & Remove Unused CSS (44 KiB savings)

You have 44 KiB of unused CSS and JavaScript. Lazy-load product images, compress hero images, and remove dead code. This shrinks your page payload and cuts your LCP from 6.6s to under 3s on mobile. Customers see your content before they leave.

Expected impact
↑ 50% faster load
↑ More sales
🔗
Add Descriptive Alt Text to All Images

Write specific alt text for every product image, hero image, and logo ("Golden retriever wearing blue collar" instead of "image123.jpg"). Google will crawl these descriptions, improving your ranking for relevant keywords. You'll also become accessible to screen reader users — expanding your audience.

Expected impact
↑ Better local search ranking
