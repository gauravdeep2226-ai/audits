What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

⚡
Zero Lazy Loading on Images & Resources
High Impact

Your site loads all images and scripts immediately without deferring offscreen content. With a 14/100 mobile score, this is catastrophic for therapy appointment booking flow. Patients accessing your Jane App integration on mobile are waiting for unnecessary resources to download before they can even see your scheduling options.

📦
No Meta Tags for Mobile Experience & Viewport Optimization
High Impact

Missing or improperly configured viewport meta tags mean your site isn't scaling correctly on mobile devices. For a healthcare provider relying on patient self-booking through Jane App, this creates friction immediately. The HTML lacks proper responsive design anchors that would make your appointment interface touch-friendly and fast-loading on phones.

🔍
Render-Blocking Resources Delaying Patient Access
High Impact

Third-party scripts and CSS are blocking page render, preventing patients from seeing your "Book Now" CTA before precious seconds tick away. For a therapy platform where patients are actively seeking to schedule appointments, every second delay is a lost conversion. This is why your FCP (First Contentful Paint) is critically slow on mobile.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

🖼️
Implement Native Lazy Loading & Image Optimization

Add loading="lazy" to all image tags below the fold and compress images using WebP format with PNG/JPG fallbacks. Your Jane App booking widget and any hero images should load instantly, with ancillary images deferring until the patient scrolls. This alone will slash your mobile LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) from over 8 seconds to under 2.5 seconds.

Expected Impact
↑ 55% faster LCP
🚀
Defer & Async Non-Critical JavaScript

Move analytics, tracking, and secondary scripts to async/defer attributes so your appointment booking form renders immediately. Keep only the Jane App integration script in the critical path. This prevents render-blocking and lets patients see your "Schedule" button within 1 second on mobile, dramatically improving interaction readiness.

Expected Impact
↑ 48% faster FCP
📱
Add Viewport Meta Tag & Mobile-First CSS

Ensure your head contains <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0"> and restructure CSS to be mobile-first with media queries for desktop. This guarantees your Jane App booking widget scales properly and touch targets are appropriately sized (minimum 44px) on phones where therapy patients are most likely to schedule appointments.

Expected Impact
↑ 42% mobile usability
