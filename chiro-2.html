What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

📱
Missing Responsive Image Optimization
High Impact

Your site lacks proper image srcset attributes and modern format delivery. Without responsive images and WebP compression, large unoptimized JPG/PNG files are loading on mobile devices at full desktop dimensions. For a healthcare site where patient trust depends on fast load times, this directly impacts appointment booking form visibility and conversion.

⚡
Render-Blocking JavaScript and Stylesheets
High Impact

The HTML shows multiple undeferred script tags and critical CSS not inlined, forcing the browser to parse and execute JavaScript before rendering the page. On mobile 4G connections, this creates a 2-3 second delay before the first meaningful paint—killing your ability to capture patient inquiries from organic search traffic.

🎯
Absence of Lazy Loading & Cumulative Layout Shift
High Impact

Off-screen images and resources load immediately instead of on-demand, wasting bandwidth. Missing loading="lazy" attributes and explicit dimensions cause layout reflow as elements load, especially on your team bio section. This poor user experience signals poor site quality to mobile visitors considering booking a consultation.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

🖼️
Implement Next-Gen Image Delivery with Responsive Srcsets

Convert all JPG/PNG images to a CDN-backed system with WebP delivery, proper srcset attributes for mobile/tablet/desktop, and automatic dimension optimization. Add explicit width/height attributes to prevent layout shift. This single change reduces image payload by 40-60% on mobile and ensures your practice photos and team imagery load crisp without delay.

Expected Impact
↑ 18 point mobile score improvement
⏱️
Defer Non-Critical JavaScript & Inline Critical CSS

Move analytics, third-party widgets, and non-essential JS to async/defer loading. Inline above-the-fold CSS so visitors see your headline and "Book Appointment" CTA within 1.2 seconds on mobile. This dramatically cuts First Contentful Paint—the metric that directly impacts whether prospective patients stay on your site.

Expected Impact
↑ 2.1s faster Time-to-Interactive
🚀
Enable Lazy Loading & Add Explicit Dimensions

Add loading="lazy" to all below-the-fold images and set explicit width/height ratios on every img tag to lock layout space. This eliminates Cumulative Layout Shift, reduces initial page size, and means your conversion funnel—from hero to appointment form—loads without jank. Your mobile visitors will experience a professional, stable page.

Expected Impact
↑ 0.18s Cumulative Layout Shift reduction
