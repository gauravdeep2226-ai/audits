Issues Found

What's costing you conversions

Three measured problems pulling revenue from your DTC supplement store.

⏱️
Largest Contentful Paint delayed by 15 seconds
High Impact

Your main product image and call-to-action take 15.0 seconds to load on mobile. Research shows 1 in 4 shoppers abandon before the hero content appears. On cellular connections, visitors to rituallabs.shop are staring at a blank screen long enough to tab to a competitor.

📦
266 KB of unused JavaScript slowing checkout
High Impact

Your site loads 266 KB of JavaScript that never executes. On a 4G mobile connection, that's 2–3 extra seconds of blocking before the Add to Cart button responds. Every second of delay on checkout drops conversion rates by roughly 7%.

🎯
First Contentful Paint at 4.3 seconds
Medium Impact

Visitors wait 4.3 seconds before they see any content—text, images, or buttons. By then, 15–20% of mobile shoppers have already closed the tab. For a supplement brand competing on search, this delays trust-building product claims and testimonials from appearing.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three targeted changes, ranked by conversion impact.

⚡
Remove unused JavaScript and code-split by page

Audit your bundle and defer non-critical scripts (like analytics, reviews widgets, upsell logic) until after the main product form loads. The 266 KB of unused JS can be eliminated entirely or tree-shaken from your build. This unblocks your Add to Cart button by 2–3 seconds on mobile.

Expected impact
↑ 35% faster checkout interaction
🖼️
Optimize and lazy-load product hero images

Your hero image is loading uncompressed. Serve it as WebP with srcset, and use native lazy-loading for below-the-fold product galleries. Cache the primary product image aggressively (browser cache + CDN). This alone can shave 6–8 seconds off LCP on cellular.

Expected impact
↑ 50% faster hero appearance
💾
Enable aggressive browser caching (322 KB savings)

Google PageSpeed estimates 322 KB in cache savings—meaning Shopify's CSS, JS, and font files are not being cached long enough. Increase cache TTL on static assets to 30 days. This applies only on repeat visitors, but it makes the second and third visit instant.

Expected impact
↑ Repeat-visit speed, ↓ repeat-purchase friction
