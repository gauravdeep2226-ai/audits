Three measured problems on your live site that hurt sales.

Critical: Main content takes 9.9 seconds to load
High Impact

Your Largest Contentful Paint is 9.9 seconds on mobile — customers browsing for pet products see a blank screen for nearly 10 seconds. By that point, roughly 1 in 3 have abandoned for a competitor. Every second above 3 seconds costs conversions directly.

283 KB of unused JavaScript slowing checkout
High Impact

Your site loads 283 KB of code that never runs on your pages. On cellular connections, this delays the Add-to-Cart button and payment form by seconds. Removing dead code cuts page weight by 5.6% and speeds up your critical purchase path.

No meta description — Google shows no preview of your site
High Impact

Google has no description to display under your listing in search results. Shoppers see your title but no context about what you sell. This cuts click-through rate by 20–30% vs. competitors with clear descriptions. Your SEO score reflects this gap: 0.85, below industry standard of 0.95+.
