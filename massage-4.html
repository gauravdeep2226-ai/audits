What's Holding You Back

3 critical areas impacting your online presence

🖼️
Unoptimized Hero and Service Images Without Modern Formats
High Impact

Your wellness center's imagery—particularly the hero section and spa/treatment photos—are likely being served as large, uncompressed JPGs or PNGs. At 31 mobile speed, heavy image payloads are directly crushing your performance. WebP and AVIF formats can reduce file sizes by 35-50% without quality loss, but your site isn't using them. For a spa business where visual presentation matters, this is especially painful because clients need to see your facilities, yet the images preventing them from even loading your site quickly.

⏱️
Render-Blocking JavaScript and Synchronous CSS Blocking First Paint
High Impact

Your site likely has JavaScript or CSS resources that are loaded synchronously in the or early , preventing the browser from rendering anything until they're fully downloaded and parsed. This directly explains your catastrophic 31 mobile score. Third-party scripts for booking systems, analytics, or review widgets are common culprits. For a wellness center where first-time visitors are often deciding whether to book an appointment, a blank screen for 3+ seconds is an appointment lost.

📱
No Lazy Loading or Image Dimension Declarations
High Impact

Images throughout your site (gallery sections, testimonials, service cards) are likely loading eagerly on page load rather than when users scroll to them. Additionally, missing width/height attributes on img elements cause layout shift, tanking your Cumulative Layout Shift score. On mobile, users see your appointment button move as images pop in, making it feel broken. This is especially damaging for spa websites where users are already on mobile browsing during downtime.

Recommendations

How We'd Fix It

3 targeted improvements for measurable results

🎨
Convert All Images to WebP/AVIF & Implement Responsive Picture Elements

Compress your entire image library to WebP (with PNG fallback) and AVIF where possible. Use elements with srcset to serve scaled versions for mobile (max 480px), tablet (max 1024px), and desktop. This alone will shave 400-600KB off your initial page load. Your hero image serving a 200KB WebP on mobile instead of a 800KB original PNG is a game-changer. Pair this with proper width/height attributes to eliminate layout shift.

Expected Impact
↑ 48% faster FCP
⚡
Defer Non-Critical JavaScript & Inline Critical CSS

Move booking widget scripts, analytics, and review plugins to the end of the body with defer or async attributes. Inline your critical CSS (above-the-fold styles for hero, header, CTA buttons) directly into the so the browser can paint your appointment CTA immediately. Lazy-load your third-party booking system after the page is interactive. This unblocks your First Contentful Paint from 4+ seconds to under 2 seconds on mobile.

Expected Impact
↑ 42% faster FCP
🔄
Add Lazy Loading & Image Dimension Attributes Across All Media

Add loading="lazy" to all below-the-fold images (gallery, testimonial photos, service cards). Set explicit width/height on every img tag to reserve space and prevent layout shift. For your appointment gallery and before/after treatment photos, this prevents the page from jerking around while images load. This is especially critical for mobile users where every pixel matters, and your Cumulative Layout Shift will drop from poor to excellent.

Expected Impact
↑ 35% improved CLS
