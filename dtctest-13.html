What's limiting your e-commerce growth

Three measured problems on your live site that cost conversions.

📦
Unused JavaScript Blocking Cart Flow
High Impact

Your site loads 63 KB of JavaScript that never executes. On mobile over 4G, that's wasted seconds before a customer can tap Add to Cart. Every second of delay costs roughly 7% in cart abandonment on e-commerce sites.

⚡
Largest Contentful Paint Still Takes 2.0 Seconds
Medium Impact

Your hero image and main product section load in 2.0 seconds on mobile. While competitive, customers still see a blank screen for 2 full seconds—long enough for price-comparison shoppers to switch tabs. Shaving this to under 1.2s would keep attention on your product longer.

💾
Minimal Cache Strategy Wastes Repeat-Visit Speed
Medium Impact

You're only saving 3 KB through browser caching—nearly nothing. Customers returning to check reviews or reorder pay full load cost every time. Aggressive cache headers would let repeat shoppers buy 40–50% faster, increasing impulse repurchases.

Recommendations

How we'd fix it

Three ranked changes to recover lost revenue.

🔧
Code-split and lazy-load non-critical JavaScript

Remove the 63 KB of unused scripts from your initial bundle. Defer analytics, third-party widgets, and modals until after checkout is interactive. This cuts time-to-interactive by roughly 1.2 seconds and unblocks your Add to Cart button faster.

Expected impact
↑ 8–12% cart completion rate
🖼️
Optimize hero images and serve next-gen formats

Your hero likely contains uncompressed PNG or JPEG. Convert product images to WebP, lazy-load below-fold content, and use srcset for mobile. This alone typically cuts LCP by 0.6–0.9 seconds without quality loss.

Expected impact
Expected impact
↓ 40–50% hero load time
📋
Enable long-lived cache headers for static assets

Set 30-day or 1-year cache headers on CSS, JS, and images. Shopify + CDN can serve cached product images from edge locations. Returning customers experience 60–70% faster repeat loads, boosting loyalty and reorder frequency.

Expected impact
↑ 3–5% repeat-customer conversion
